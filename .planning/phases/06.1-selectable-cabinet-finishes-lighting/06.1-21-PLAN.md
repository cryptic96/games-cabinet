# Plan 06.1-21: Release v0.8.0 with the cabinet looks, after the owner approves the verified draft

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6.1`): "From the owner pages, the owner picks the cabinet's finish for every visitor from seven furniture looks, each with its own wall, and switches warm cubby lighting on, optionally only at night by each visitor's own clock, plus a cubby-depth look."

Coverage note: no SPEC; the spec-less probe fallback was skipped visibly; no probe-derived Edge Coverage or Prohibition predicates exist for this run.

<objective>
Ship the phase as release v0.8.0 through the established path (pull request, checks, the owner's merge approval, attested tag draft verified on the workstation, owner-approved publish, pull-based deploy) and confirm on the container that the release is healthy and still shows today's look until the owner publishes a new one (D-25; CONTEXT "Review flow": a release the owner approves after the draft check).

Purpose: the looks reach the server safely, and the owner can then choose one from the live owner host.
Output: v0.8.0 on the container, sanitised evidence in the SUMMARY.

Plan shape (why five tasks and two owner checkpoints): the same shape the previous release plans ran successfully. The two checkpoints gate two different irreversible acts at two different moments: first the owner approves merging into `main` and pushing the tag (nothing exists to verify yet); only after the attested draft exists and Claude has told the owner it is verified does the owner approve the deploy environment, which publishes. Asking for both at once would make the owner approve a publish before the draft was checked.
</objective>
