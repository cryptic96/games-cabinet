# Phase 06.1 Plan 17: Choosing by sight Summary

The look panel's Design and Wood groups are now tiles that draw themselves: each design tile is the whole piece (top, one cubby with one box, base or legs) in the trial wood, and each wood tile is the top-left corner of a piece on the trial design, both built from the real cabinet hooks with their own five attributes and no inline style.

## What was built

- **Tiles (`Settings.cshtml`):** two `fieldset.pic-choices.look-choices` groups with a `.look-tiles` row of twelve `label.pic-tile.look-tile`. Each swatch is a `span.look-swatch` (`aria-hidden`, own `data-design`, `data-wood`, `data-backs`, `data-lights="off"`, `data-depth`) holding `cabinet > section > floor, body > cubby > placement, top, trim, base`, all spans so the markup stays valid inside a label. Names and sub-lines come from `CabinetLook` and `OwnerStrings` as the copy contract words them. A `Preview time` fieldset (Day checked, Evening) sits after Depth, natively disabled unless the stored lights are Evenings only, with its hint. The no-script note is a visible paragraph the script hides.
- **Styles (`owner.css`):** swatch numbers on their own classes; the picture box is a size container with `aspect-ratio: 150 / 128` capped at `--tile-h`; design swatch sizes the section by both limits through `--section-ratio`; wood swatch crops with `overflow: hidden`. Below 40rem each group is one sideways row (`scroll-snap-type: x proximity`, 7rem tiles, 8 px gap, `scroll-padding-inline: 16px`); from 40rem an `auto-fill` grid; the sticky phone preview gains the 1 px `--paper-edge` bottom edge as a box-shadow layer under the paper spread.
- **Script (`owner-look.js`):** `applySwatchAttributes` writes the trial wood, backs and depth on design swatches and the trial design, backs and depth on wood swatches (lights stay off); Preview time sets or removes `data-evening` on the preview wrapper only, toggles the fieldset's `disabled`, swaps the hint, and calls `refreshLamp(preview)` after every trial or Preview time change; the preview is marked `data-settled` after its first draw; a Preview time change starts no trial and clears no status.
- **Strings:** `PreviewTimeLegend`, `PreviewDay`, `PreviewEvening`, `PreviewTimeHint`, `PreviewTimeHintDisabled` in `OwnerStrings.cs`; the two hints in `owner-format.js`.

### Plan and contract interpretation (follow the Round outcome where they disagree)

- **Caption layout:** the contract puts the radio beside a 16 px name. At 7rem a word such as `Mahogany` cannot stay whole beside a 24 px radio in DejaVu (the first build broke it into `Mah/ogan/y`), so the radio sits above the name in every tile. The name is 14 px from 64rem, where the tiles are narrower.
- **Five woods in one row at 1440:** the two-column layout leaves a controls column of about 31rem, which cannot hold five 7rem tiles with a 24 px gap. From 64rem the grid uses `minmax(5rem, 1fr)`, an 8 px gap and 6 px tile padding so the five woods share one row and the seven designs take five plus two. From 40rem to 64rem the contract grid is unchanged.
- **Picture height follows the tile width:** the picture box keeps the mock-up's 150 x 128 proportion up to `--tile-h` (128 px, 112 px under 40rem), so a 7rem tile shows a piece that fills it instead of a small piece on a tall wall; it reaches the full `--tile-h` when the tile is wide enough.
- **No-script note:** plain markup hidden by the script instead of a `noscript` element, as the plan's step says (a `noscript` is hidden text in a script-off test context).
- **Preview time and the lamp wiring** landed with the tiles commit because the script reads those parts; its tests and the phone layout are in the second commit.
- **R-2 (lights only):** Preview time shows only the lit cubbies; no room dimming exists to switch.
- **Tracer gate:** autonomous form, see Verification.
