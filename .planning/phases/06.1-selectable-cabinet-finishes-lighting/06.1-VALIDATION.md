## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** the affected project's quick command (`Cabinet.UnitTests` filtered by trait, or `node --test` on the touched file)
- **After every plan wave:** unit + integration + all `node --test`, plus the browser classes the wave touched under the DejaVu fontconfig
- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** full suite green, A/B default-identity green on samples 65 and 400 at 1440 and 390, CI browser job green
- **Max feedback latency:** 60 seconds for the quick command
