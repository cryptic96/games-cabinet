# Phase 06.1 Plan 08: The cabinet stylesheets in four layers, with today's look frozen and the light hook in place Summary

The cabinet is now drawn from four ordered CSS layers (structure, design, wood, modifiers) with Classic, warm brown, wood backs, Shaded and lights Off as the layers' base case, every tint a wood token of identical value, public chrome reading room tokens, and the renderer's floor and light hooks in place; the default look stays at zero differing pixels.

## What was built

- **Order statement:** `site.css` opens with `@layer structure, design, wood, modifiers;`. The cabinet page and the owner Pictures page link `cabinet.css` then the three layer files (each with `asp-append-version`) before `card.css`.
- **Structure (`cabinet.css`):** all former structural rules inside `@layer structure`, plus the stacking map (section light 1, boxes 2, top/trim/base 2 by tree order, shade over boxes 3, focused and open boxes 4, floor hook -1; nothing on `.cubby` or `.section-body` creates a stacking context), `.section-light` (positioned like the body, `display: none`, `opacity: 0`), `.light-pool` (positioned by the cubby's own `--x --y --w --h --frame`), `.section-floor`, the section's `--bw --bh --section-ratio` with `aspect-ratio` reading the ratio, the wall shadow as the section's own background (`--room-shade-rgb` at `--wall-shadow`), the box keyline ring in every box shadow, the 6 px focus halo geometry, and `--base-overhang` in the base box.
- **Design (`cabinet-design.css`):** Classic sizes (`.cabinet` block) and every member recipe (grain, sweeps, back planks with seams gated by `--seam-k`, board tones, trim, top, base, arch, floor shadow). No colour literal anywhere; colours come through `rgb(var(--...-rgb) / a)` and `var(--...)`.
- **Wood (`cabinet-wood.css`):** one base block on `:root, [data-wood]` with the warm brown values and the full vocabulary: board and lit edge (`--wood`, `--wood-lit`), `--back`, `--back-painted` (`#0f2025`), recess, ply, inlay, the Deep wall and floor tokens, every colour mix as a token, `--shade-rgb` / `--shade-deep-rgb` / `--box-shade-rgb` (`20 10 4`), sheen, plank lift, highlight, neutral black and white, arch colours and alphas, arris, grain, `--keyline`, `--ring-halo`, `--wall-shadow`, lamp colours and strengths, falloff colour, room and chrome tokens, floor and skirting. The grain strength per section member is set here as a unitless strength.
- **Modifiers (`cabinet-modifiers.css`):** the Shaded default (`--shade-alpha` 0.20, `--shade-depth` 20 %) and the neutral strengths `--depth-k` and `--seam-k`.
- **Chrome (`site.css`):** body paints `--room`, `--room-text` and the page falloff (`--room-falloff`, `no-repeat top / 100% 100lvh`); header, status, sync block, language toggle, skip link, games list and the page's own focus rings read the room tokens with today's tokens as fallbacks. Owner chrome, the owner banner and the card are untouched.
- **Renderer (`render.js`):** each section appends `section-floor` before its body and `section-light` after `section-base`; the light layer holds one `light-pool` per cubby carrying that cubby's four numbers through `style.setProperty`. No look attribute is read or named.
- **Tests:** `cabinet-layers.test.mjs` (15 checks: order statement, nothing outside a layer, wood layer custom properties only and no lengths, no colour in the design layer, neutral colours only in structure and modifiers, no pair or root selectors, exact z-index map, no stacking context on cubby or body, channel tokens declared, no wood name shared with an unlayered `:root` and none is `--frame`, hairlines at least 1 px, chrome focus rule and room tokens, light hidden by default); the pinned plinth-lip assertion in `cabinet-css.test.mjs` now reads its tokenised form with `--plank-lift-rgb` declared as `255 235 210`; `page-scripts.test.mjs` reads the four layers and checks the section children, the pool numbers, the empty-section case and that `render.js` names no look attribute.

## Deviations from Plan

Where the plan text and the Round outcome or the approved mock sources disagree, the latter were followed.

### Round outcome over plan text

**1. [Round outcome] Section light is positioned like the body, not `inset: 0`**
- The contract and the approved mock place `section-light` exactly like `.section-body`, with pools offset by `--frame` and the floor glow reaching past the box; the dusk shade that `inset: 0` was meant to hold no longer exists.
- **Files:** `cabinet.css`

**2. [Round outcome] No `--dusk-rgb`, no dusk layer**
- Lights only (no room dimming) drops the dusk token the plan listed in the wood vocabulary.

**3. [Round outcome] `section-floor` hook added, no `data-position`**
- The floor patch per piece won, so the renderer appends `section-floor` before the body and the structure layer places it behind the body (level -1, `inset: 0`). Sections stay separate, so no `data-position`.
- **Files:** `render.js`, `cabinet.css`

**4. [Round outcome] Falloff painted on `body`**
- `body` carries `--room-falloff` as a top-anchored `no-repeat` `100% 100lvh` background; no fixed layer, no `html::before`, never `background-attachment: fixed`. Brown's value is `none`.

**5. [Round outcome] Token names**
- `--wood-lit` instead of a wood-layer `--wood-edge`; `--box-shade-rgb` constant for the over-box shade; no wood-layer name shares a name with an unlayered `:root` token (the test enforces it).

### Auto-fixed issues

**6. [Rule 1 - Bug] The chrome focus rule outranked the card's own rings**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (full browser suite: two keyboard-ring tests failed)
- **Issue:** the Round outcome's `button:not(.placement):focus-visible` raises the specificity of the chrome ring from (0,1,1) to (0,2,1), above the card row ring.
- **Fix:** `button:where(:not(.placement)):focus-visible` keeps specificity at (0,1,1) and still excludes boxes.
- **Files modified:** `site.css`. **Commit:** 8f47092

**7. [Rule 1 - Bug] Cascade flips from layering**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** two unlayered `site.css` rules used to lose to later same-specificity `cabinet.css` rules and would have started winning once `cabinet.css` was layered: `.cabinet-retry` (padding, font size) and `.cabinet-filling` (margins).
- **Fix:** removed the dead duplicate `.cabinet-retry` rule and the dead margin declarations from `site.css`, and moved `cursor: pointer` into the structure layer's rule, so the computed result is exactly today's.
- **Files modified:** `site.css`, `cabinet.css`. **Commit:** 8f47092

**8. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Owner Pictures page uses box styles**
- **Issue:** `Pictures.cshtml` links `cabinet.css` for `.placement`; without the wood layer `--box-shade-rgb` would be undefined and the box shadow would drop out.
- **Fix:** it links the three layer files too.
- **Files modified:** `Pictures.cshtml`. **Commit:** 8f47092

**9. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] `--room-on-accent`**
- The retry button and the sample switcher put text on the accent; on a light wall the accent is the ink colour, so ink text would vanish. Added `--room-on-accent` (brown: `var(--ink)`, identical to today) and read it there. A light-wall wood must set it to its wall colour.
- **Files modified:** `cabinet-wood.css`, `cabinet.css`, `site.css`. **Commit:** 8456cae

### Interpretation notes

- The open-box outline (reduced motion) reads `--room-text` instead of `--wall-text`; identical on brown, readable on a light wall.
- The wall shadow follows the approved mock (a soft band down the right side, inside the side allowance); the prose in the contract also mentions a thinner band under the top overhang, which the mock does not draw.
