# Phase 6.1 Plan 06: The round's outcome becomes the contract Summary

The owner's two answers are now the design contract: the UI-SPEC "Round outcome" resolves every round item and every changed value as final, backed by a second mock-up round that was re-measured after the independent critic pass. Nothing outside `.planning/` was touched, and no look CSS was built in the product.

## What was done

1. **Task 1 (tracer):** wrote the "Round outcome" section into the UI-SPEC and marked the approved sheets in the round document (`742023a`).
2. **Task 2:** built the second round in a scratch copy of the first round's layered sources: seven look sheets (desktop, phone, evening, crops), comparison sheets (lamp, R-10, kicks, depth, falloff, tiles, walnut, 35 pairs, focus rings), measurements and the zero-pixel baseline, persisted into `ui-refs/mockup-round-2/` with machine paths scrubbed (`4634b8f`).

Applied the ten critic fixes (K1 to K10) and the keyline, re-measured and re-shot (`8d0ccac`), then recorded the answer verbatim in section 6 of the round document and turned every pending UI-SPEC row into a final value (`fd9c6a7`).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Birch warm fill strength 0.25 instead of the critic's 0.35**
- **Found during:** Task 3, K3 re-measure
- **Issue:** with 0.35 the birch peak at Shaded measured 1.28, under 1.3 (and Flat 1.14); on painted-on-birch the fill and the reduced shade also pulled the peak to 1.27
- **Fix:** fill 0.25 and multiplied by `--depth-k` (it vanishes at Flat, where there is no shelf shadow to cancel); `--fill-k` and `--back-shade-k` reset on painted backs
- **Files modified:** `ui-refs/mockup-round-2/source/modifiers.css`, `wood.css`, `design.css`
- **Commit:** 8d0ccac

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Keyline colour and birch exception**
- **Found during:** Task 3, K2 measurement
- **Issue:** the keyline in the wood's own deep shade tint was too faint on a painted back on birch (32 of 396 box tops under 1.3) and drew outlines on birch's pale back
- **Fix:** the keyline reads the constant over-box shade colour, and birch's wood back sets `--keyline-lit` 0
- **Commit:** 8d0ccac

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] The critic's visibility numbers needed a shade-aware method**
- **Found during:** Task 3, K1 re-measure
- **Issue:** the round's own measurement compared the unshaded box tone with the back, which at Shaded reads 1.07 to 1.22 on dark backs although a visitor sees the shaded box top (1.32 to 1.36)
- **Fix:** replaced the table with the shade-aware method at three depths; recorded the method in the contract
- **Commit:** fd9c6a7
