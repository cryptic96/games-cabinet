## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

The executor found eleven issues while porting the looks onto the real layers. F1 to F6 and F9 were fixed in the mock. F7, F8, F10 and F11 are carried into the build as contract changes. All are written up in the round document (section 6).

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] F1: a fixed falloff layer made Chrome draw text with grey anti-aliasing**
- **Issue:** Text edges changed by up to 115 of 255 and broke the frozen default.
- **Fix:** The layer exists only when the wood needs it (`none` on warm brown). The Critic recommends moving the falloff onto the page (C17), which the owner must still confirm.

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] F2: the planned stacking map changed today's paint order**
- **Issue:** A box's shadow painted over the side boards (3,071 pixels, up to 12 of 255).
- **Fix:** Top, sides and base share level 2 with the boxes and stay above by tree order. The build must follow this exactly.

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] F3: mixing colours for the dusk, even at 0 %, changed rounding (22 pixels)**
- **Fix:** The dusk mix is applied only in the evening rule. Moot now that room dimming is dropped.

**4. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] F4: Classic writes ten literal tints, not five**
- **Fix:** Tokens added for each (`--arch-top-rgb`, `--arch-bottom-rgb`, `--neutral-dark-rgb`, `--neutral-light-rgb` and the tone mixes).

**5. [Rule 1 - Bug] F5: token name clash with unlayered `--wood-edge` and `--ink` on the root**
- **Fix:** The lit edge is named `--wood-lit`.

**6. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] F6: wood tint reached box faces through the shade, dropping Rust under 4.5:1 on birch and oak**
- **Fix:** A constant `--box-shade-rgb` (`20 10 4`) for the over-box layer gives 4.51 on every wood. The contrast test must read that token.

**7. [Carried to build] F7: a dusk layer sized to the section box tints the wall inside the box**
- Replaced by a token mix; moot with lights only.

**8. [Carried to build] F8: the visibility table is optimistic by 0.09 to 0.15 on wooden backs**
- The test must add plank lift, sheen and every light layer reaching the peak (Critic C10).

**9. [Rule 1 - Bug] F9: furniture dusk on a light wood at 0.25 looks muddy**
- Lowered to 0.12 on birch in the mock. Moot with lights only.

**10. [Carried to build] F10: legs are measured from the carcass edge, not from the section box**
- The two readings of the contract differ by the 32 mm allowance.

**11. [Carried to build] F11: Deep needs one extra node per cubby for the inner walls**
- Plus a floor hook per section and light hooks. The Critic notes this is six layers per cubby, one over the contract (C13).
