# Phase 06.1 Plan 10: Five woods, each with its room and wall, and the right logo on every wall Summary

Walnut, oak, birch and mahogany join warm brown as complete woods with the owner-approved values, each bringing its own wall, falloff, floor and skirting from the first paint; the official dark logo shows on the two light walls and the white one everywhere else, and unit tests that read the shipped tokens guard the chrome contrast, logo choice, focus rings, back tones and kick per wood.

## What was built

- **Wood layer (`cabinet-wood.css`):** `[data-wood="walnut"]`, `"oak"`, `"birch"` and `"mahogany"` blocks next to the warm brown base. Each holds board, lit edge, back, recess pair, cubby tones, shade tints, grain, arris, arch alphas, lamp strengths and the room: wall, text, accent, falloff alpha and gradient, floor, skirting, wall shadow and room shade tints. Final values come from the Round outcome: walnut wall `#d2ccc0` (not plum), kick `#2e1c12` / `#3a2418`, walnut and oak backs `#261811` / `#221a12`, birch kick `#4a4337` with recess-low `#5f574a`, birch pool 0.12, back shade 0.35, fill 1, no keyline when lit, birch and oak ink ring halo. No dusk tokens exist (lights only). Only custom properties and unitless strengths; no lengths.
- **Room (`cabinet.css`, structure layer):** the `section-floor` hook now paints the piece's floor patch: a 36 mm skirting band behind the base whose lower edge is 14 mm above the feet line, a lit top line and a shadow line, and a floor tone fading to the wall and out towards the bottom of the section box. It reads `--skirting`, `--floor`, `--room-shade-rgb` and `--highlight-rgb`, scaled by `--room-k`, so warm brown (room-k 0, no floor, no skirting) draws nothing visible and stays pixel-identical. The falloff stays painted on `body` by the earlier layer work.
- **Logo:** `powered-by-bgg-dark.svg` copied unchanged from the official pack (SHA-256 `527357ef...82d8cc`). `_Layout.cshtml` sets a `LightWall` view flag from `CabinetLook.HasLightWall`; `_Footer.cshtml` renders the dark or white logo markup (same alt, size and link, `asp-append-version` kept). The owner layout sets nothing, so owner pages always get the white logo. `NOTICE.md` lists both files with their hashes and says both are copied unchanged; no local path appears in it.
- **Chrome (`site.css`):** `.bgg-credit` reads `--room-text`, so a logo that fails to load leaves its link and alt text in the wall's text colour.
- **Tests:**
  - `StyleSheetTokens` reads custom properties per selector block (through `@layer` and `@container`), resolves `var()` with fallbacks, `color-mix` in srgb and in oklch with a grey, numbers and channel triples, blends on gamma-encoded channels, and gives WCAG luminance; `StyleSheetTokensTests` covers it on an inline sample.
  - `LookChromeContrastTests` (per wood): text and muted text at least 4.5 and line at least 3 on the wall under the brightest falloff; label on accent at least 4.5; every wall below 0.12 or above 0.45 luminance; logo by the 0.27 rule equals `HasLightWall` and reaches 4.5; the page's ring at least 3 on its wall; the box ring reaches 3 on frame/shelf, plain back and painted back through the accent or the ink halo; every back in a safe luminance zone; kick darker than the wall (and on light walls darker than the front by 1.4, 4.5 against the wall, never black). The measured walnut and birch kick numbers match the Round outcome (0.0146, 10.18:1, 1.60:1 and 0.0575, 6.77:1, 5.94:1).
  - `BrandAssetTests` pins the dark logo hash and the notice wording.
  - `LookWoodTests` (browser): stored oak shows its wall, board and body-painted falloff (no fixed layer, no `html::before`, no overflow); the floor patch lies inside every section box; every wood's wall and logo file, size, alt and link, with `/owner/settings`, `/owner/pictures` and `/owner/locations` keeping today's wall and the white logo; the card keeps its paper on walnut and birch; the load-error message and Try again button read on all five walls (ink background on the light ones); a failed logo keeps its link and alt text in the wall text colour.

## Deviations from Plan

Where plan text and the Round outcome or the approved mock sources disagree, the latter were followed.

### Round outcome over plan text

**1. [Round outcome] The falloff is painted on `body`, not on `html::before`**
- The plan's tracer behaviour and artifact list describe a fixed `html::before` layer `100lvh` tall. The owner's second answer (S-1: yes) replaced it with a top-anchored, `no-repeat`, `100% 100lvh` body background and forbids a fixed layer. The earlier layer work already paints it that way; the tracer test asserts the new contract (body background-size equals the large viewport height, `html` has no background, `::before` is `none`, no fixed full-viewport element, no overflow).
- **Files:** `Cabinet.BrowserTests/LookWoodTests.cs`

**2. [Round outcome] No dusk tokens, no dusk-alpha or light-wall dim tests**
- Lights only (R-2): the plan's dusk alphas and the evening column of the chrome test do not exist; evening equals day. No `--dusk-*` token was added.

**3. [Orchestrator note] `--room-on-accent`, set to the wall colour on light walls**
- The plan names a new `--room-accent-text` token holding the paper colour on a light wall. The previous plan already added `--room-on-accent` (read by the retry button and the sample switcher), and the orchestrator's note says a light-wall wood sets it to its wall colour, so walnut and birch set `--room-on-accent` to `#d2ccc0` and `#ddd6ca`. The browser test checks the button background is ink on light walls and the label reaches 4.5:1 (the wall colour on ink is well above that). `.cabinet-retry` already read these tokens, so `cabinet.css` needed no change for it.

**4. [Placement] The floor patch lives in `cabinet.css` (structure layer), not in the design layer**
- The approved mock draws it in its design sheet, but the design sheet is owned by the designs plan running in parallel, and the plan lists `cabinet.css`. It reads only tokens and `--bh`, `--floor-margin`, `--u`, so it works for every design that sets those.

### Auto-fixed issues

**5. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] `.bgg-credit` had no colour**
- **Issue:** the footer link inherited the browser's link blue when its image failed, which breaks the E6 error state (alt text in `--room-text`).
- **Fix:** `color: var(--room-text, var(--wall-text))` on `.bgg-credit` in `site.css`. With the image loaded nothing paints differently; the identity test stays at zero.
- **Files modified:** `Cabinet.Service/wwwroot/css/site.css`. **Commit:** bca5416

**6. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Fingerprinted logo file names in the browser test**
- Static assets are served as `powered-by-bgg.<hash>.svg`; the test strips the hash before comparing file names.

### Additions

- The measured muted and line ratios differ slightly from the Round outcome table (for example walnut muted 5.45 vs 5.12, oak line 4.27 vs 4.53) because the table was measured by the mock's own method; every value clears its threshold, so no token was changed.
