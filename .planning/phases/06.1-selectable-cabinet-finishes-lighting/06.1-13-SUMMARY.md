# Phase 06.1 Plan 13: The owner tries a look on a real cabinet preview, then uses it for everyone Summary

The Settings page now shows a real cabinet section (two rows of three cubbies, nine invented boxes, one face-out cover, one empty cubby) drawn by the real `renderCabinet` and the cabinet layers inside a wrapper that carries the trial look; every tap changes only that wrapper, the summary lines and the buttons, and `Use for everyone` posts the five values once, with Undo, while `Back to current` drops the trial.

## What was built

- **Sample layout:** `js/owner-look-sample.json`, a desktop-profile layout (section 940 x 620 mm, frame 20, six 300-high cubbies in two rows, palette equal to the cabinet's twelve tones, titles `Lantern & Harbour`, `Quiet Quarry`, `Night Market`, `Copper Canal` and five more invented ones). The `js/owner-` prefix makes it owner-only; the public port answers an empty 404.
- **Markup (`Settings.cshtml`):** a `Head` section links the four cabinet layers; `section#finish` holds `.look-layout` with the stage (the `figure.look-preview` with five attributes, `aria-hidden` and `inert`, a `.cabinet` mount holding an empty stand-in section, the `Visitors see:` line, an empty `Trying:` line, a `noscript` note) and the controls (the unchanged no-script form with `look-choice` labels, the primary and a hidden-until-script `Back to current` button, the status line, the server Undo form). The helper line is now the contract's longer sentence.
- **Script (`owner-look.js`):** `initLookPanel(doc, deps)` keeps `current` (from the wrapper) and `trial` (from the radios); a change writes the five attributes on the wrapper, the `Trying` line, the `aria-disabled` state of both buttons and the status; publish posts `design`, `wood`, `backs`, `lights`, `depth` through `postOwner` (revision and token header included) and on `saved` updates `Visitors see`, shows the saved line with `Undo` and keeps focus on `Use for everyone`; Undo posts `?handler=Undo` with the publish revision and puts the earlier look back; failures keep the trial with the contract text and `Reload` where the shared helpers give it; discard moves focus to the checked design radio. A sample that cannot load shows the preview-failure line on the trial wall and announces it, and the controls keep working. Exports `readLook`, `writeLook`, `sameLook`, `applyLookAttributes`, `readLookAttributes`, `loadSampleLayout`.
- **Strings:** `owner-format.js` gains the look statuses, `Visitors see` and `Trying` templates, the preview-failure line, `LOOK_NAMES`, `LOOK_FIELDS` and `lookSummary`; `OwnerStrings.cs` gets the no-script note, the longer helper and the `Back to current` label. A node test checks the script wording against the server file and the display names against `CabinetLook.cs`.
- **Styles (`owner.css`):** the old `.finish-tile`, `.finish-swatch`, `.finish-note` and `.finish-form + .row-status` rules are gone; added `.look-layout` (one column, two columns from 64rem with `minmax(0, 28rem)` and `minmax(0, 1fr)`, 32 px gap), `.look-preview` (4 px padding, own room from the wood tokens, `overflow: hidden; contain: paint`, sticky on a phone and in the stage from 64rem, static in short landscape), the section sizing `min(100%, calc(var(--preview-h) * var(--section-ratio)))` with `--preview-h` `min(46dvh, 28rem)` and `min(70dvh, 36rem)` from 64rem, the stand-in numbers, `.look-choice` (44 px, 24 px native radio, 3 px inset ring when checked) and the dashed disabled primary button.
