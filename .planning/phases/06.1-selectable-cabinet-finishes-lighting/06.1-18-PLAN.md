# Plan 06.1-18: A new design never scrolls the visitor away - in Chrome and in Safari's engine

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6.1`): "From the owner pages, the owner picks the cabinet's finish for every visitor from seven furniture looks, each with its own wall, and switches warm cubby lighting on, optionally only at night by each visitor's own clock, plus a cubby-depth look."

Coverage note: no SPEC; the spec-less probe fallback was skipped visibly; no probe-derived Edge Coverage or Prohibition predicates exist for this run.

<objective>
Complete the live switch (D-23, D-24; owner decision P4-A): keep the section in view where it was when a design change alters section heights, with native anchoring switched off during the change; defer a change that arrives during a pull-out; prove it in Chromium and WebKit, with the logic also tested in Node, and add the WebKit step to CI.

Purpose: designs reserve different heights; nobody watching the cabinet is scrolled away when the owner switches the design.
Output: the scroll keeper and deferral in `look-switch.js`, Node tests, Chromium and WebKit browser tests, the CI step.
</objective>
