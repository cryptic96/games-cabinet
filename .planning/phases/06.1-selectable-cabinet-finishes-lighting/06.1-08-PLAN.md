# Plan 06.1-08: The cabinet stylesheets in four layers, with today's look frozen and the light hook in place

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6.1`): "From the owner pages, the owner picks the cabinet's finish for every visitor from seven furniture looks, each with its own wall, and switches warm cubby lighting on, optionally only at night by each visitor's own clock, plus a cubby-depth look."

Coverage note: no SPEC; the spec-less probe fallback was skipped visibly; no probe-derived Edge Coverage or Prohibition predicates exist for this run.

<objective>
Split the cabinet CSS into the four ordered layers the design contract fixes (D-04): structure, design, wood and modifiers, one file each, with Classic and warm brown as the layers' base case, every tint moved into a wood token of identical value, public chrome reading room tokens, the full structure layer (stacking map, the light layer's box, the wall shadow and the focus halo geometry), and the renderer's light hooks (D-11). The default-identity test from plan 06.1-01 proves after every step that today's look has not moved by one pixel (D-05).

Purpose: from here, a wood, a design or a modifier is added by writing its own layer only, and nothing else can shift.
Output: three new stylesheets, a layered `cabinet.css`, the order statement in `site.css`, the hooks in `render.js`, and the CSS shape tests.
</objective>
