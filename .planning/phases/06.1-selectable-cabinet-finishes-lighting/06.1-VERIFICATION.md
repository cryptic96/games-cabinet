## Scope note: "seven finishes" became designs times woods

The roadmap text says "seven furniture looks" and "seven finishes". The phase context (D-01, D-02) changed this, on the owner's decision, to seven designs times five woods plus an optional painted-backs extra, with each wood bringing its own wall. The seven looks the owner approved are specific combinations, and every one of them is reachable (and approved by sight in the two mock-up rounds and the build review). I treated this as the owner-approved shape of the roadmap goal, not as a gap. It is the reason success criterion 2 reads "by rendered swatch" as design tiles and wood tiles, each previewing itself.
