# Phase 06.1 Plan 19: Every combination keeps its geometry, stays in its box and keeps its focus ring Summary

The whole look matrix is now proved by browser tests: only the design moves anything, nothing a design, wood, back, depth or lamp draws leaves its section box (a floor tail on four woods was found and fixed), a box face is the same pixel with the lights off and on everywhere, a focused box keeps its whole ring at both section edges on every wood and back, and an empty collection still stands as one bare section on every wall.

## What was built

- **`LookGeometryTests` (new, 29 cases).** For each of the 7 designs at 1440 and 390 px on the 65 and 400 samples, one in-page loop writes `data-wood` (5), `data-backs` (2), `data-depth` (3) and five light states (off, on before the fade, on lit, evenings by day, evenings at night with the evening and lit markers) onto `<html>`: **150 states per design**, and after each one reads the box of every section and cubby plus the page's scroll size. Every state must read exactly what the first read, and the page must never scroll sideways. A teeth case injects a rule that moves one wood and expects exactly that wood's 30 states reported.
- **`LookContainmentTests` (extended, 65 cases, was 14).**
  - The existing design x lights case is unchanged in meaning, but its edge ring is now compared with the pixel three pixels outside the box instead of the flat body colour.
  - 35 design x wood pairs, each at 1440 and 390 px on sample 65, with Deep, lights lit, and **both** wood and painted backs (140 sets of sections): every part's rectangle lies inside the section's rectangle, the one-pixel edge ring (left, right, bottom whole; top everywhere except under and beside the topmost member) equals the wall outside within 3 of 255, and the page does not scroll sideways.
  - Light order: 7 designs x 5 woods x 3 depths (105 states): the face of every spine and flat box, inset 3 px, is identical lit and unlit while the back does change.
  - Empty collection (sample 0): 7 designs x 5 woods x 2 widths (70 states): exactly one section, bare cubbies, no box, contained, and the status line's computed colour equals `--room-text`.
  - Two teeth cases: a tint that reaches the box edge is reported; a light laid over the boxes (`z-index` above them) is reported as changing their faces.
- **`LookFocusTests` (new, 16 cases).**
  - 5 woods x 2 widths: for every design and both kinds of back (140 focus states), the leftmost and the rightmost box of the first section is focused with the keyboard heuristic on: `:focus-visible` matches, the computed outline is `rgb(217, 185, 138)`, solid, 2 px, offset 2 px, and the ring is present on all four sides (at least 95 % of positions along each side) in a picture of the box's neighbourhood. On oak and birch the halo is in the box shadow (6 px, the ink colour) and the ring stands between two bands of ink; on the other woods the halo is transparent. The ring against the painted back is at least 3:1.
  - 5 walls: the language links, the collection link and the sync button show a 2 px solid ring at offset 2 px in the wall's `--room-ring`, whole on four sides, and that colour reaches 3:1 against the wall.
  - A teeth case: a cubby that clips what is drawn outside it makes the left ring of the edge box report as not whole.
- **CSS fix (found by the matrix).** In Deep with the lights lit, the bottom pixel row of the section box differed from the wall by 4 to 5 of 255 on the oak, mahogany, walnut and birch walls, because the floor glow (`cabinet-modifiers.css`) and the floor tone (`cabinet.css`) both faded to nothing exactly at the box edge, which cut a faint tail. Both now reach nothing at 90 % of their height; the glow's strength went from 0.14 to 0.155 to keep its overall brightness (the existing lit-oak glow test would otherwise have lost its margin). Warm brown has no floor and no glow, so the default look is untouched (identity test: zero pixels).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The floor glow and the floor tone left a faint tail on the box's bottom row**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (the new Deep and lit matrix)
- **Issue:** On oak, mahogany, walnut and birch the last pixel row of the section box was 4 to 5 of 255 away from the wall outside (the Round outcome allows 3), only on the bottom edge and only where a wood has a floor.
- **Fix:** The floor tone and the floor glow reach nothing at 90 % of their height (was 96 % and 100 %); the glow strength 0.14 to 0.155 keeps its mean. A start value in the modifiers layer, not a contract value.
- **Files modified:** `Cabinet.Service/wwwroot/css/cabinet.css`, `Cabinet.Service/wwwroot/css/cabinet-modifiers.css`
- **Commit:** 7cd2738

### Plan text that the Round outcome overrides (recorded, not bugs)

**2. [Plan vs Round outcome] No dusk or evening dimension.** The plan counts `evenings` measured both day and evening and a dimming round state; the Round outcome says lights only (R-2), so evening equals day for looks. The evening and lit markers are still written in the geometry loop (150 states per design, not 90) because the lit marker changes what is displayed; no containment case adds a dusk state.

**3. [Plan vs Round outcome] Real counts.** The plan's "all 35 pairs ... 90 combinations" held, but the measured counts are: geometry 7 designs x 4 views x 150 states = 4200 state reads; containment 35 pairs x 2 widths x 2 backs = 140 section sets; light order 105 states; empty 70 states; focus 140 box states (280 box rings) and 20 wall-control rings.

**4. [Plan vs Round outcome] Edge ring against the wall just outside.** The existing containment case compared the ring with the body's flat background colour. Walls with a falloff (walnut, birch at 0.30) carry a soft gradient under the first section, so every edge ring now compares with the pixel three pixels outside the box. The top edge keeps the Round outcome's exclusion beside the topmost member.

**5. [Plan vs Round outcome] Focus ring colour and halo woods.** Checked as `#d9b98a` on every wood x backs pair, 3:1 against the painted back (the Round outcome); on oak and birch the ring colour is the colour of the pale frame, so presence is proved by the ink bands on both sides of it rather than by a before and after difference.
