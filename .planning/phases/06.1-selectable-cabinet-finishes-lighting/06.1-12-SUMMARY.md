# Phase 06.1 Plan 12: Cubby depth (flat, shaded or deep) and painted backs on any design Summary

Depth is its own setting in the modifiers layer: Flat draws no shading, Shaded stays the base case, and merged Deep draws a ceiling, two side walls and a floor as four gradient layers on the cubby's own background plus a lit top face on every shelf board; painted backs paint the back one petrol colour with no planks while keeping the shade of the shelf above.

## What was built

- **Deep (`cabinet-modifiers.css`):** `[data-depth="deep"] .cabinet` sets the over-box shade to 0.12. `[data-depth="deep"] .cubby` fills the design's `--deep-img`, `--deep-size` and `--deep-pos` slots with the ceiling, left wall, right wall and floor from the wall tokens (`--cubby-top`, `--cubby-left`, `--cubby-right`, `--cubby-floor-back`, `--cubby-floor-front`) sized by `--depth-inset` 16, `--depth-ceil` 8 and `--depth-floor` 24. `[data-depth="deep"] .cubby::before` fills `--top-face-img` and `--top-face-size` with the 6 mm lit top face, its hairline drawn with `max(1px, ...)`. Everything is custom properties; no layout size is set.
- **Flat:** `[data-depth="flat"]` sets `--depth-k: 0` (the back's top and side shade vanish) and `[data-depth="flat"] .cabinet` sets `--shade-alpha: 0`. The planks, grain and lit edges are the design's and stay.
- **Painted backs:** `[data-backs="painted"]` sets `--seam-k: 0`, `--back: var(--back-painted)`, `--back-shade-k: 1`, `--pool-k: var(--pool-k-painted, 1)`, `--fill-k: 0` and `--keyline-lit: 0.4`. The shade of the shelf above and down the sides stays and follows the depth choice. In Deep the walls and floor stay wood.
- **Design layer slots (`cabinet-design.css`):** the cubby's background list gained a first slot (`--deep-img`, default `none`) and the shelf board's a first slot (`--top-face-img`, default `none`), each with its size, position and repeat entries. With the defaults the pixels do not change.
- **Browser tests (`LookDepthTests.cs`):** a stored Deep look (seeded through the owner data file) draws the walls, reports a computed shade of 0.12, keeps every section and cubby rectangle equal to Shaded's and paints nothing outside its section; a theory over the seven designs at 1440 and 390 steps through every depth x back pair and checks the rectangles and the one-pixel edge ring (left, right and bottom against three pixels outside, at most 3 of 255); Flat has a computed shade of 0, an unshaded top and visible plank seams; a painted back reads exactly the painted colour across the middle of every cubby at every depth, keeps the top shade in Shaded and Deep and keeps wood walls in Deep.
- **Node tests (`cabinet-layers.test.mjs`, 5 checks):** the shade alphas are 0, 0.20 and 0.12 and none is above the palette cap (read from the Domain source); flat and painted set their strengths and come after the defaults they replace; Deep is four gradient layers on the cubby and one on each shelf board with no clip-path and no extra element; the modifiers layer sets no size, position, margin, padding or display outside the light layer; depth and back selectors name one choice each.

## Deviations from Plan

Where the plan text and the Round outcome disagree, the Round outcome was followed.

### Round outcome over plan text

**1. [Round outcome] Deep is the merged construction, drawn through the cubby's own slot**
- The plan allowed a `clip-path` polygon and up to five layers; the Round outcome fixes four layers, no clip-path, and asks for the K10 approach. It worked, so there is no `cubby-deco` element.
- **Files:** `cabinet-modifiers.css`, `cabinet-design.css`. **Commit:** c5e2067

**2. [Round outcome] Painted back strengths**
- The plan text asked for "a top shadow `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)` fading by 50 %". The Round outcome has the painted back use the standard back shade (`--back-shade-k` reset to 1), one petrol for every wood, `--pool-k` 1 and the keyline 0.4 while lit, so those are set. The plan's `--back-painted` per-wood alternative was not needed.
- **Files:** `cabinet-modifiers.css`. **Commit:** f469cbf

**3. [Round outcome] Deep sizes and shade**
- Ceiling 8 mm, floor 24 mm, side walls 16 mm to 70 % and the Deep shade 0.12 come from the Round outcome (the earlier 16 mm floor and ceiling are superseded); the sizes are the design layer's existing `--depth-*` tokens.

### Auto-fixed issues

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The design layer needed two background slots**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** a later layer cannot add a gradient to a background list a design already wrote, and no design exposed one. Deep on the cubby's own background therefore needs a slot in the design layer, which the plan's file list does not name.
- **Fix:** added `--deep-img` / `--deep-size` / `--deep-pos` to the cubby and `--top-face-img` / `--top-face-size` to the shelf board in `cabinet-design.css`, defaulting to `none` / `auto`, copied from the approved mock port. Designs that only change sizes, tokens or back construction keep working; a design that rewrites the cubby background list wholesale must keep the first slot.
- **Files modified:** `cabinet-design.css`. **Commit:** c5e2067

**5. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The page's CSP blocks an injected style element**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (test run)
- **Fix:** the test hides the boxes with an adopted constructable stylesheet instead of a style tag.
- **Files modified:** `LookDepthTests.cs`. **Commit:** c5e2067

### Interpretation notes

- The tracer test seeds the stored Deep look through the owner data file, as the plan describes. The theory and the Flat and painted tests change the look attributes on the root element at run time instead of starting a host per combination: every rule is attribute-driven, so this proves the same CSS in 84 combinations without 84 host starts.
- A gradient dithers by one level between rows in Chrome, so equality checks on gradient pixels allow a difference of 2; the seam check asks for a spread of more than 12 across a cubby's middle row.
- The lamp's extra strength on the Deep top face and the shelf-board lighting belong to the lights work and are not drawn here; `--keyline-lit` is set for painted backs but nothing reads it until the lights are built.
- The walnut, oak, birch and mahogany walls are not checked here (the wood layer is being filled in another plan); the tests run on brown, the base wood, and the colour tokens are read as they come.
