## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 2`): "A deterministic, natural-looking cabinet layout, built on synthetic data while the BGG approval is pending, that the owner has reviewed and approved visually from an empty cabinet up to several hundred games, on desktop and phone widths."

<objective>
Change how boxes stack, as the owner decided after reviewing the expansion families (D-21 to D-24): piles go largest at the bottom with no overhang, big boxes may lie flat instead of opening a mostly empty section (a server setting), thick expansions stand upright beside their base game with a line naming it, and expansion stacks go thickest at the bottom.

Purpose: the owner saw a wide box overhanging empty air on top of narrower ones, and a 65-item cabinet spilling into three sections because big boxes did not fit the short rows. This plan runs before the phone section and golden layouts (02-06), so those record the new arrangement once.
Output: ordered piles and stacks, the lie-flat fallback with its setting, upright expansions end to end (engine, JSON, renderer, CSS), invariant and stability tests including the new documented exception, before and after measurements, and screenshots for review.
</objective>
