## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 2`): "A deterministic, natural-looking cabinet layout, built on synthetic data while the BGG approval is pending, that the owner has reviewed and approved visually from an empty cabinet up to several hundred games, on desktop and phone widths."

<objective>
Make the boxes and the furniture look real: per-game palette tones with guaranteed text contrast, generated covers (pattern plus title plate), spine and flat-box detailing, readable shortened labels, hover and focus rules from the UI contract, and the owner's chosen furniture finish, "B classic" (D-20): grain on every board, lit shelf edges with a stable tone per shelf, a moulded overhanging top, thicker-reading side boards, planked backs and a plinth with an arched opening between two feet over a floor shadow.

Purpose: the owner judges the prototype visually. After seeing the tracer, the owner found the furniture itself plain, so this slice makes the cabinet read as a crafted piece of wooden furniture full of game boxes rather than coloured rectangles in a grid. The generated cover also becomes the permanent fallback when real box art is missing.
Output: palette, shade-cap and label code in Domain, palette, pattern and furniture hooks in the renderer, the section box and the classic finish in CSS, contrast (including under the furniture's shade) and label tests, and a screenshot-based visual check kept outside the repository.
</objective>
