## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 2`): "A deterministic, natural-looking cabinet layout, built on synthetic data while the BGG approval is pending, that the owner has reviewed and approved visually from an empty cabinet up to several hundred games, on desktop and phone widths."

<objective>
Bring the cabinet to phones with its own narrow section design and readability floors derived from the real gutter and frame, make every design self-checking (box limits, validator, worst-case family), and lock the whole engine down with recorded layouts and a version-bump guard before the owner's review rounds start tuning it.

Purpose: phones are where friends look at the cabinet on game night; and once tuning starts every arrangement change must be a conscious, visible diff.
Output: `ReadabilityFloor`, the phone design, design limits and validation, floors applied in the arrangement, oversize boxes in the samples, phone, design and golden tests, recorded layouts and a docs section on re-recording.
</objective>
