## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 2`): "A deterministic, natural-looking cabinet layout, built on synthetic data while the BGG approval is pending, that the owner has reviewed and approved visually from an empty cabinet up to several hundred games, on desktop and phone widths."

<objective>
Add expansion families as the owner sketched them: a flat stack of thin sideways expansions beside each base game, "+N more" when the stack is full, orphan expansions as their own labelled boxes, with the stack column reserved when the base is placed so arrival order never strands an expansion (owner decision D-19).

Purpose: the real collection is about 65 items including expansions; without families the cabinet would scatter expansions across the shelves.
Output: `StackLayout`, family-aware engine and arrangement, synthetic families and orphans in the samples, layer, marker and orphan rendering, and family layout and stability tests including the documented first-expansion exception.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-17 | Denial of service | very large families | low | mitigate | The column width is fixed and `StackLayout` is linear in the family size, capped by `ExpansionStackMax`; a family of hundreds of expansions costs one pass and renders one marker |
