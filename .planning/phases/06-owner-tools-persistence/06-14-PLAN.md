# Plan 06-14: Tracer for pictures - choosing a candidate changes the cover for every visitor

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
The pictures tracer and the first slice of the pictures area: the owner picks `Main picture` (or `Owned edition`, or back to `Automatic`) on the owner port's Pictures page and every visitor's cabinet and card show it, through the overlay's forced pick in the mapper, the version, the owner-data choice and the page's choice form (IMG-02; D-11, D-12, D-26).

Purpose: the core of picture overrides, before tiles, worth-a-look, links and marks build on it.
Output: forced pick and version in the mapper, `PictureEdits.Choose`, the Pictures page with its choice forms, tests.
</objective>
