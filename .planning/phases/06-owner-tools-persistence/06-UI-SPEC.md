## Spacing Scale

Unchanged scale for page chrome (multiples of 4: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64).

| Token | Value | Usage on the owner pages |
|-------|-------|--------------------------|
| xs | 4px | Gap between an icon and its text; gap inside a tag; row-status top margin |
| sm | 8px | Tile padding; gap between buttons; gap between tiles on phone; input padding |
| md | 16px | Row padding on phone; gap between rows; panel padding on phone; dock padding inline |
| lg | 24px | Row and panel padding on desktop; gap between tiles on desktop; dialog padding |
| xl | 32px | Gap between panels and sections; heading top margin |
| 2xl | 48px | Space between the last panel and the footer |
| 3xl | 64px | The dock's height (`--dock-h`) |

**Exceptions (all intentional):**

- **Strokes and offsets are not spacing:** the 2px focus outline and its 2px offset, the 3px inset selected-tile ring, the 3px `text-underline-offset` and the 1px to 4px borders are strokes and offsets, not gaps, and are not on the scale.
- **Tap targets (FIXED):** every control is at least **44px** tall and wide on **every pointer** (owner pages are phone first). Visible checkboxes and radios are 24px inside a 44px label. A tile or row is its own tap area.
- **`--tile-h: 128px`** picture area on desktop, **112px** on phone (`max-width: 40rem`). Multiples of 4. A tile is as wide as its picture's true proportion needs, up to the grid column.
- **`--thumb: 48px`**: the small cover in a locations row.
- **Page width:** `.owner-main` is `min(var(--panel-max), 100%)`, centred (64rem is a measure, not a gap).
- **Dialog width:** `min(28rem, 100vw - 32px)`.
- **Safe area:** the dock adds `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to its padding, and the games list adds `calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` of bottom padding so the last row clears the dock.
- Page gutters, header and footer keep their earlier values (8px phone body padding, 24px desktop).
- The card's `--ruling` (28px) is untouched and used only by the Edit link row, which follows the existing "Tap rows" rule.

No new sizes and no new weights: exactly **3 fixed page sizes (24, 16, 14)** and **2 weights (400, 600)**.

| Surface | Ring |
|---------|------|
| Wall, wood-mid bar, strip, dock, skip link | 2px solid `--wood-light` (accent), 2px offset. 4.6:1 on `--wood-mid`, 7.8:1 on `--wood-dark` |

### Error and status treatment

- Error text: `--ink`, 16px, preceded by a 4px solid `--paper-label` start border and a **semibold lead sentence** (the lead names the problem; the rest says what to do). - Success and neutral status ("Saved"): `--paper-label` 14px text with the `Undo` button inline.
- Destructive warning line (above Delete location and Restore file): `--ink` text with a 4px start border in `--danger` and a semibold lead.

- A unit test asserts, with the same gamma-correct blend as the card test, that `--ink` and `--paper-label` pass 4.5:1 on `--paper` and on `--hover-tint`; that `--paper` passes 4.5:1 on `--danger`; that `--wall-text` passes 4.5:1 on `--wood-mid` and on `--wood-dark`; and that `--ink` passes 4.5:1 on `--wall-text`.
- A unit test asserts the corner tag's two tones (`--paper` fill, `--ink` edge) reach 3:1 for at least one tone against **every** palette background (computed minimum across the palette is 5.0:1: Rust is the tightest).

### Worth a look: the rule (Claude's discretion, fixed here)

A game is a **candidate** when any of these holds, and it is **to check** until resolved:

1. The automatic pick is not the owned edition's picture **while an owned-edition picture exists** (the override worklist rule). Games with no owned-edition picture at all are **not** candidates for this reason (they have nothing else to show).
2. The picture is missing or broken (no usable art, or the stored picture failed to load; the existing `data-art="failed"` count).
3. The mark **Picture changed on BGG** (the chosen kind follows BGG and BGG replaced the picture of that kind).
4. The mark **Link no longer works** (the linked picture failed to fetch; its stored copy still shows).

A candidate is **resolved** when the owner records **Looks right** (keep automatic, reviewed) or makes an explicit choice other than Automatic (Owned edition, Main picture, Your link). Resolution is invalidated, and the row returns, when either candidate picture's content changes on BGG, or when marks 3 or 4 arise. `{n}` in the counter is the number of unresolved candidates and `{m}` is the number of candidates (resolved ones stay counted while their resolution is valid), so the counter counts down and a returning row raises it again.

Resolved rows leave the list on the next page load. **In the current page a resolved row stays where it is** (so focus and Undo are not lost) with a `Checked` tag, and the counter updates at once.

## Interaction

### Save and undo (the one model for the whole phase)

1. **Every control saves at once** (radio, rename on `change`, add, delete, move, Looks right, Use this, Remove link; a **select saves its settled value**, see "Games"). There is no page-level Save button when script runs.
2. **The status line:** after a save the **row's own** `.row-status` (`role="status"`, in the row, directly after the control group so Tab reaches `Undo` next) reads `Saved` (or the specific text in the copy tables) with an `Undo` button (secondary, 44px). For a bulk move the **dock's** status does it. SR text for the row status carries the context: `Saved<span class="visually-hidden"> picture choice for {title}</span>`.
3. **`Undo` reverts the latest owner change only.** It stays on the row until the owner makes **any other change** (anywhere on the page) or reloads; at that moment the earlier row's status drops its `Undo` and keeps the plain text. A second tab or a stale page gets `Can't undo because something changed after this.` and no data changes. Undo removes the log entry it reverts.
4. **Save failed:** `Couldn't save that. Nothing changed. Try again.` in the same status line, no Undo, the control returns to its previous value. **Out of date** (the data changed under this page): `This page is out of date. Reload it and try again.` with a `Reload` button. **Expired form** (antiforgery): `This page has expired. Reload it and try again.` with `Reload`.
5. A stored link and its copy are never dropped by switching tiles; only `Remove link`, replacing the link or the 30-day expiry drops them (D-13). `Undo` of a removal or a replacement fetches the link again.
