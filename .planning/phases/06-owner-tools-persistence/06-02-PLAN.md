# Plan 06-02: The owner host's road through the firewall and the reverse proxy

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
Give the owner port a road from home: the firewall template admits it from the reverse proxy only, the Traefik example gains the owner router, the env example documents the off-by-default switch and ranges, the selfcheck proves the walls on the host, and a scripted route check proves both routers from the owner's network (D-01, D-02, D-28).

Purpose: the outer two walls (Traefik allow-list and nftables) and the proof that they and the app's walls agree on the deployed host.
Output: updated deploy templates and their tests, a route-check script with its stubbed test, deploy docs.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Internet to Traefik | Hostnames and client addresses arrive here; the owner router keeps the LAN and VPN allow-list permanently |
| Traefik to the container | Only the Traefik address may reach ports 5080 and 5082 (nftables) |
| Repository templates to the real hosts | Templates are copied and filled by the owner; real values never come back |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-02-01 | Elevation of privilege | owner port reachable from other hosts | high | mitigate | nftables rule admits 5082 only from `@CABINET_TRAEFIK_IP@`; default-drop; render test counts exactly three tcp rules |
| T-06-02-02 | Elevation of privilege | owner tools exposed when the public router drops its allow-list at go-public | high | mitigate | Separate owner router and hostname with its own permanent allow-list, documented in the example header and docs |
| T-06-02-03 | Spoofing | client-supplied X-Forwarded-For passed through Traefik | high | mitigate | Docs and example forbid `notAppendXForwardedFor` and client networks in `trustedIPs`; app reads only the last entry |
| T-06-02-04 | Information disclosure | real hostnames or ranges committed in templates, docs or tests | high | mitigate | Placeholders only; env lines commented out; lint and review |
| T-06-02-05 | Information disclosure | selfcheck or route check printing addresses or hostnames | medium | mitigate | Output is PASS/FAIL and status codes; tests assert no hostname in output |
| T-06-02-06 | Denial of service | release deployed before the owner prepared ranges | low | mitigate | Owner endpoint stays off unless the env lines are set; app start-up fails closed when set without ranges or proxy |
</threat_model>
