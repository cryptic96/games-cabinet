# Plan 06-08: Tracer for owner data - a location written once shows on every visitor's card

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
The phase's data tracer: a location written through the single owner-data writer reaches every visitor's card and survives a restart, through every data layer at once: the owner-data model, its file store, the single writer, the overlay on the snapshot, and the one publisher of the collection state that the sync now also goes through (D-16, D-18, D-21, D-22, D-26 version).

Purpose: proves the owner-data architecture end to end; the next plan adds the owner page's forms and the writer's rules on top.
Output: owner-data records and store, `OwnerDataService`, `CollectionPublisher`, the overlay, and their tests.
</objective>
