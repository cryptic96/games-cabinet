# Plan 06-01: Tracer - the owner port answers only at home

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
Open the owner tools' own door: a second Kestrel port that carries the owner pages and nothing else carries, two independent walls inside the app (listener port and proxy-resolved client range), fail-closed start-up, and the test factory's third port, proven end to end with the first owner page (D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04; orchestrator decision 3). Per D-03 the network location is the only lock: no password, login, account or session is added.

Purpose: every later owner feature lives behind this door; it is the costly-to-reverse piece of the phase, so it is proven first.
Output: the owner port, the gates, the owner folder convention, a first Settings page in a first owner layout, the factory's owner port and client, wall tests.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| LAN or VPN client to Traefik to owner port | Client-controlled headers (Host, X-Forwarded-For) and paths cross here |
| Known proxy to app | The only peer whose forwarded client address is believed |
| Configuration (env file) to app start-up | Owner endpoint, ranges and known proxy are read once |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-01-01 | Elevation of privilege | owner routes on the public port | high | mitigate | `UseOwnerOnlyRoutes` (metadata, LocalPort) plus `UseOwnerPathGuard` (first path segment); wall tests; path tricks pinned in the wall-hardening plan |
| T-06-01-02 | Spoofing / Elevation | missing known proxy turns the proxy address into the client | high | mitigate | `FromConfiguration` refuses an owner endpoint without a known proxy or without ranges |
| T-06-01-03 | Tampering (CSRF) | owner forms in later plans | medium | mitigate | Antiforgery configured here: SameSite Strict, HttpOnly, Secure in Production, header token |
| T-06-01-04 | Denial of service | large owner posts | low | mitigate | 64 KB request limit on every owner page through the folder convention |
| T-06-01-05 | Spoofing | same-LAN guest editing (no password) | low | accept | Owner's recorded decision (network location is the only lock); every change is logged and exportable |
</threat_model>
