# Plan 06-22: Backups - download the owner data, the reminder banner, the change log and local times

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
Give the owner the backup they chose: a readable download of all owner data, a banner on every owner page that counts changes since the last download and holds the button, the recent-changes log with a download divider, and viewer-local times (D-23, D-24, D-27; R7; I-A; orchestrator decision 5; research open question 8: the export includes the change log).

Purpose: OWN-02's "it is backed up", as the owner chose it (export and import only).
Output: export writer, download handler and bookkeeping, the banner, the Settings backup and change-log panels, `owner-settings.js`, tests.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-22-01 | Information disclosure | download endpoint reachable publicly | high | mitigate | Owner-only page, port wall, antiforgery post; integration test on the public port |
| T-06-22-02 | Tampering (CSRF) | a cross-site post triggering a download | medium | mitigate | Antiforgery token required; SameSite Strict cookie |
| T-06-22-03 | Information disclosure | change log or banner on public pages | medium | mitigate | Rendered only by `_OwnerLayout` on the owner port; test |
