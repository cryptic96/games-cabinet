# Plan 06-16: The picture tiles - four mounted photos per game, saved with one tap and an Undo

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 6`): "From the home network or VPN only, the owner can correct box images (and, if BGG cannot supply storage locations, define and assign them), with that data surviving syncs, restarts and releases and being backed up."

<objective>
Turn the Pictures page's choice forms into the UI-SPEC's radio tiles: four tiles per game showing the processed picture the cabinet would use, in true proportions, with verdict tags and the "Automatic's pick" hint, saving at once with Undo and once per keyboard pass (D-15; R3; U3).

Purpose: the owner sees every candidate as it would look and switches with one tap.
Output: `PictureRows`, the tiles' markup, `owner-pictures.js`, tile styles, the generated-cover export, tests.
</objective>
