## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 3`): "The owner's real BGG owned collection appears automatically in the deployed cabinet, kept fresh by a polite hourly sync and a guarded 'sync now', and the site keeps working when BGG does not."

<objective>
Build the BGG doubles from the folded todo (D-20): a local-only fake BGG with scenario switches, synthetic XML in the measured response shape, and a scripted HTTP handler for tests. The sync built next is exercised against these, never against real BGG.

Purpose: every sync behaviour and failure state must be reproducible locally and in CI without touching BGG or a real token.
Output: the `Cabinet.FakeBgg` project, its tests, solution and test-project references, and a development guide section.
</objective>
