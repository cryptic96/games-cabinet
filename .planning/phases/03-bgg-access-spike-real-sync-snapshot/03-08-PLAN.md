## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 3`): "The owner's real BGG owned collection appears automatically in the deployed cabinet, kept fresh by a polite hourly sync and a guarded 'sync now', and the site keeps working when BGG does not."

<objective>
Make sync now fair and the collection fresh on its own: one shared, persisted 10-minute window opened by every sync start (D-05, D-21), a status endpoint that tells pages when the collection was last synced and what the button may do, and an hourly scheduler with a guarded start-up run (SYNC-01, SYNC-02, SYNC-03).

Purpose: visitors may press sync now, but BGG sees at most one sync per window and roughly one per hour otherwise.
Output: sync state and its store, sync settings, cooldown in the coordinator, the scheduler, the status payload and endpoint, the factory opt-in for background runs, tests and docs.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-29 | Denial of service | BGG traffic from presses | high | mitigate | One global window opened by every start and persisted before enqueue; single-flight; tests cover restart, failed run and concurrent presses |
