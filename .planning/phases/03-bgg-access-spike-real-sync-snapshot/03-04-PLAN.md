## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 3`): "The owner's real BGG owned collection appears automatically in the deployed cabinet, kept fresh by a polite hourly sync and a guarded 'sync now', and the site keeps working when BGG does not."

<objective>

Purpose: BGG's licence requires a linked, legible credit on every public page; one shared layout makes it impossible for a page to forget it.
Output: `_ViewStart.cshtml`, `Shared/_Layout.cshtml`, the slimmed `Index.cshtml`, footer credit styles, the official logo asset, and tests.
</objective>
