Purpose: the button and its feedback are how visitors use the guarded sync; the redraw built here is reused by the live updates.
Output: the press behaviour in `sync.js`, pure state functions in `status.js`, copy strings, `redraw()` in `cabinet.js`, node tests and a server-contract test.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-49 | Denial of service | press loops from the page | medium | mitigate | A non-pressable button sends no request; polling only after this page's accepted press, every 5 s, capped at 10 minutes; the server's global window bounds BGG traffic regardless |
| T-03-50 | Tampering | status values rendered as text | medium | mitigate | Only fixed copy strings and formatted numbers or dates are written, through `textContent`; markup-building APIs are forbidden (grep) |
| T-03-51 | Information disclosure | outcome detail | low | mitigate | One generic failure sentence for every failure category; no counts in the held-back sentence |
</threat_model>
