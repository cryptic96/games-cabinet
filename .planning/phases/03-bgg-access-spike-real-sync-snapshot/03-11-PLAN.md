## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 3`): "The owner's real BGG owned collection appears automatically in the deployed cabinet, kept fresh by a polite hourly sync and a guarded 'sync now', and the site keeps working when BGG does not."

<objective>
Tell visitors how fresh the cabinet is: a compact "Synced ... ago" line under the title with the exact local time on hover or tap (D-06, D-08, D-22), and a calm "showing the last sync from ..." note when syncs have not gone through for 3 hours or a result is held back (D-13). The pure logic is tested in CI with `node --test` (D-23).

Purpose: SYNC-03 and the SYNC-04 note; the sync button's behaviour follows in the next plan on top of this markup.
Output: server-rendered status line and stale note, `status.js`, `sync.js`, copy strings, styles for the whole sync block, a shared case table, C# and JavaScript tests, and a CI step.
</objective>
