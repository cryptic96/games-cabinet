# Plan 05-13: The open card holds still during a sync, and the phone sheet drags to close

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 5`): "A visitor can tap any box, watch it slide out of the shelf, and read everything needed to pick it for game night in English or Dutch, by touch, mouse, keyboard or screen reader."

<objective>
Make the open card behave in real use: a sync that lands while a card is open waits until the box is back on the shelf (D-06), a phone rotation or resize across the breakpoint closes the card cleanly, and the phone sheet closes with a drag from its grip or header without ever closing while the content scrolls (D-04, R5, S1).

Purpose: the last behaviour pieces of DET-01 on phones and during live updates.
Output: the close gate and drag logic in `card-flow.js` and `detail.js`, the sync gate and profile handling in `cabinet.js`, the drag styles, node and browser tests.
</objective>
