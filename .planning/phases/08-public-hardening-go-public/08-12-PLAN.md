# Plan 08-12: The measurement release, part two - host settings first, then the deploy, then the container's walls applied and proven

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: the established deploy path of plan 07-47 plus the server-side ordering of research "Pitfall 4". This plan lifts two prohibitions.

<objective>
Put the host settings in place in the right order and prove the container's walls on the real host: the owner adds `AllowedHosts` and checks it on the running version, then publishes the verified draft; Claude confirms the install, adds the outbound keys to `provision.conf`, re-runs provisioning from the active release (unit limits and outbound block), and proves with the selfcheck and real traffic that the home network is refused while sync, pictures, deploys, Sigstore and package updates still work.

Purpose: the measurements in plans 08-13 to 08-15 run on the walls as shipped.
Output: `08-SERVER-STEPS.md` (placeholders only), sanitised evidence in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
