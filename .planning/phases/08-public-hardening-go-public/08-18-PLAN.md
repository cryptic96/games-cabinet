# Plan 08-18: The release that goes public, part one - the one-way `v1.0.0` decision and a verified draft

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: D-10 step 1, rated one-way in the context: the reversibility gate is the decision checkpoint in Task 2, directly before the tag task, which carries the one-way rating. Same release path as plans 08-11 and 07-46.

<objective>
Take the hardening with the decided numbers to a verified `v1.0.0` draft: pull request, checks watched by the orchestrator, the owner's explicit one-way decision on merging and tagging `v1.0.0`, the attested draft verified on the workstation, and the owner told plainly that it is verified. Deploying and verifying on the LAN-only route follow in plans 08-19 and 08-20.

Purpose: the version that goes public is fixed and checkable before anything is opened.
Output: the merged pull request, tag `v1.0.0`, a verified draft, sanitised evidence in the SUMMARY.
</objective>
