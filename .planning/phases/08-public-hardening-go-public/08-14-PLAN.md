# Plan 08-14: The heaviest work the app does, measured on the real container

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: the first half of D-08 (peaks of a normal sync and a forced picture rebuild) and the cap proposal of D-07; the flood half of D-08 follows in plan 08-15. This plan lifts two prohibitions.

<objective>
With the owner's go, measure the CPU and memory peaks of one normal sync and one full picture rebuild on the real container without touching the snapshot or the owner data, then propose container and unit caps (peak plus headroom) for the owner.

Purpose: SEC-06's caps are sized from real image-processing and sync load, not from guesses.
Output: the "Sync and picture rebuild" section of `08-MEASUREMENTS.md`, `08-CAPS-REVIEW.md` sent to the owner.
</objective>

<interfaces>
- Container: `ssh cabinet-lxc` (sudo; never read the env file). State directory `/var/lib/cabinet` holds `snapshot.json`, `owner-data.json`, `sync-state.json`, `owner-data-versions/` and `art/`. A picture record is fetched again whenever any of its files is missing from `art/`; `ArtCache` recreates `art/` as the service user on start; unreferenced files are pruned only after a 7-day grace.
- Measuring: `systemctl show cabinet -p MemoryCurrent -p MemoryPeak -p CPUUsageNSec -p NRestarts`; `/sys/fs/cgroup/system.slice/cabinet.service/memory.peak`, `memory.events` (`high`, `max`, `oom`, `oom_kill`), `cpu.stat` (`usage_usec`, `throttled_usec`); `/sys/fs/cgroup/cpu.pressure` and `memory.pressure`; `systemd-cgtop -b -n 2 -d 5`. Peak CPU is sampled from `cpu.stat` `usage_usec` deltas once a second during a run. `memory.peak` starts over when the unit restarts.
- Sync: `Images:MaxDownloadsPerRun` 80, `Images:DownloadGapMilliseconds` 1000, `Sync:ManualCooldownMinutes` 10, start-up sync within `Sync:StartupJitterMaxSeconds` 120 after a start; status on `http://127.0.0.1:5080/cabinet/status`; a press is `curl -s -X POST http://127.0.0.1:5080/cabinet/sync` (from loopback on the container).
- Current shipped unit limits (plan 08-02): `MemoryHigh=450M`, `MemoryMax=600M`, `MemorySwapMax=0`, `CPUQuota=80%`, `CPUWeight=50`, `TasksMax=512`. Container today (lxcfs): 1 core, 1024 MB, 512 MB swap. The proxy container: 1 core, 512 MB. The CPU weight of either container is visible only on the Proxmox host (`pct config`).
</interfaces>

Repository rules: numbers and plain descriptions only in `.planning` files; never commit to `main`. Batches of proposals go to the owner as a labelled document sent as a file.
</context>
