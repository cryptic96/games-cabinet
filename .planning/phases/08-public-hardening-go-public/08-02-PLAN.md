# Plan 08-02: The container's own walls in the repository - unit caps and the outbound block

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: this plan lifts two prohibitions. **Flagged assumption (edge probe, SEC-06 `unclassified`, unresolved):** the probe could not classify SEC-06. The plan treats D-07 (two levels of caps, unit below container), D-08 (caps from measured peaks plus headroom) and D-09 (the pass bar) as its contract: the unit numbers here are starting values only, decided from measurements in plan 08-16, written into the repository in plan 08-17 and verified by the floods in plans 08-15 and 08-20.

<objective>
Ship the two container-side walls as repository templates with tests (D-07, D-15): resource limits and the GC mode on `cabinet.service`, and an nftables output chain that refuses new connections to the home network except DNS to the host's resolver. Extend provisioning (keys, validators, rendering) and `cabinet-selfcheck` (limits, egress, DNS) so the walls can be applied and proven on the container.

Purpose: a flood cannot take the whole container's memory or CPU from SSH and the deploy poller, and a compromised public app cannot reach anything on the LAN.
Output: the unit directives, the output chain, three provision keys with validators and tests, three selfcheck checks with tests. Applying them on the container happens in plan 08-12; the numbers are replaced in plan 08-17.
</objective>
