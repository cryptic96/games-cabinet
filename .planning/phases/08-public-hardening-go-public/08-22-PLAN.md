# Plan 08-22: Go - the owner signs off and the public route opens

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: D-10 step 3 and the switch of D-13, rated one-way in the context (once open, anyone may fetch and cache the site; re-adding the allow-list closes it again but does not undo what was fetched). The reversibility gate is the decision checkpoint in Task 2, directly before the switch task, which carries the one-way rating. Lifts SEC-04 concurrency for the edit and two prohibitions. **Open item (edge probe, SEC-01 `unclassified`)**: no probe edge was resolved for SEC-01; the plan treats D-10, D-11 and D-13 as its contract and closes the item with a concrete check before the go question (Task 1, step 3: real client addresses reach the proxy), confirmed after the switch by the phone in plan 08-23.
