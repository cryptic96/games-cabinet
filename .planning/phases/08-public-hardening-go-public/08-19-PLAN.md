# Plan 08-19: The release that goes public, part two - deployed, provisioned and proven on the LAN-only route

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 8`): "The site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS through the existing reverse proxy, resilient to traffic floods and unable to starve the other guests on the host, and the owner opens it only after a go-public checklist passes."

Coverage note: D-10 step 2 (everything checked on the still LAN-only route) for headers, limits with a burst, caps and the browser under the CSP; the verification flood follows in plan 08-20. Same deploy path as plans 08-12 and 07-47.

</objective>
