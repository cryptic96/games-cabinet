## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Make the art work reviewable on a developer machine: the local fake BGG serves synthetic box art from its own origin, points every picture address at itself, and assigns pictures so every case the review needs appears; the cabinet accepts that origin only in Development.

Purpose: the local review round (D-04's first stage, the layout phase's review pattern) needs real-looking art without any real image or any BGG call; the production allowlist must stay closed.
Output: the fake image route, picture assignment, the Development-only origin setting, tests and developer docs.
</objective>
