## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Enrich every owned game from BGG's `thing` call inside the sync, politely and progressively: new games at once, everything else about weekly, in bounded batches that never fail the sync (D-17). Store the details the detail card will need, and use the expansion links to stand every owned expansion beside its owned base game (D-14, D-15, D-16).

Purpose: SYNC-06 and success criterion 1 ("real expansions sit beside their owned base games in the deployed cabinet"); the details also feed the size estimate and the main-image candidate in later plans.
Output: `GameDetails`, the parser, the client, the planner, the pairing, the sync step, settings, fake-BGG data, tests and docs.
</objective>
