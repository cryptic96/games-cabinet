## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Build the two pure decision functions the art work rests on, test-first: the spine colour pair (true to the art, lightness nudged only when the title needs it, white or black text at 4.5:1 under the furniture shade) and the image choice (detector verdict from stored features, then the candidate table). Both live in the Domain, take plain values, and run in milliseconds, so every later tuning round only changes settings.

Purpose: CAB-03 legibility and IMG-01's choice are contracts with exact numbers (D-02, D-03, D-11, D-12); pinning them before any pixels flow keeps the sync and the renderer simple.
Output: `RgbColour`, `SpineColour`, `ArtFeatures`, `ArtThresholds`, `ArtVerdict`, `ArtVerdicts`, `ArtPick`, `ArtChooser`, with tests.
</objective>
