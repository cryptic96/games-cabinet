## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Run the owner's review on the real collection (D-04, D-05, D-13): build the review sheet on the server, take the cover-share screenshots of the deployed cabinet, send both to the owner as images, and tune the read-time settings through a systemd drop-in until the owner is happy overall; collect any code-level requests for the final release.

Purpose: success criterion 3 says the picks are "checked against the owner's real Dutch editions"; this is that check, and it also picks the committed cover-share default.
Output: the owner's decisions (as numbers and setting values), tuned values live on the server, a list of code-level changes for the final plan; nothing committed.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- The deployed cabinet stays on the home-network or VPN route during the review (the public route opens only in the hardening phase), so screenshots are taken from the workstation over that route.
- Round 2 (after the round-1 fixes in plans 17 and 18) runs on release v0.4.1: before the fresh sheet and screenshots, set the drop-in to `Layout__CoverSharePercent=33` (the owner's round-1 pick, D-13) and reach the deployed cabinet through the owner-approved read-only GET relay (a small workstation relay that answers each GET with `ssh cabinet-lxc curl -s -i http://127.0.0.1:5080<path>`, never a POST) instead of the SSH local forward, which the container's sshd refuses.
- Code-level tuning (analysis constants, class dimensions, stylesheet) waits for the final release; raising `ArtProcessor.AnalysisVersion` there makes the server re-process stored pictures.
