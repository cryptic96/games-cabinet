## Defects found in round 1 (by sheet row number only)

A. **Orientation bug, confirmed in code (row 49).**
   - `BoxFromVersion.TryMap` always makes the longer side the height.
   - `BoxShape.Resolve` only reshapes from a flat cover when `Disagrees(...)` is true, and `Disagrees` compares orientation-free ratios (`RatioOf` = min/max).
   - So real dimensions with the same proportions as a LANDSCAPE flat cover stay portrait, and `Art:OrientFromCover` never applies.
   - Fix: a landscape flat cover turns the real-size box to landscape even when the proportions agree. Keep the size source "real size", the area and the depth. Decide explicitly whether the oversize and lie-flat logic uses the turned front; the pose height never comes from a picture.

B. **Clearly landscape pictures that are not judged flat never orient the box** (rows 14, 25, 27, 44, 47: drawn tall with big bars). Choose a safe rule, for example:
   - an unsure (not 3D) picture whose aspect is clearly landscape beyond a margin may orient the box but not reshape it;
   - a 3D shot never orients.
   Make it a validated register setting included in the rules fingerprint if needed.

C. **The flat-vs-3D detector misjudges real pictures in both directions.**
   - Box photos on a white or (likely) transparent background are judged flat with score 0.00 (rows 8, 9, 13, 28, 42). This causes wrong picks where a flat main picture existed (rows 8, 9, 13).
   - Flat covers with dark or black edges are judged 3D shot with score 1.00 (rows 1, 4, 25, 34).
   - Root-cause first: how do `ArtProcessor`, `ArtAnalysis` and `BackdropMask` treat alpha? The working copy is resized premultiplied and read back as straight colours; are fully transparent pixels counted as backdrop? And how do dark uniform borders on a flat cover become backdrop?
   - Reproduce both failure shapes with synthetic fixtures in `SyntheticArt`:
     - a perspective box on a transparent PNG with a soft shadow;
     - a box photo on pure and near-white with JPEG-like noise;
     - a flat cover with a thick dark border and dark art near its edges.
   - Fix it so those classify correctly while the existing fixtures and the 16 signed-off review cases keep their verdicts.
   - Raise `ArtProcessor.AnalysisVersion` so stored pictures are re-analysed. A version bump makes the records due again (re-download), capped at 80 per run, so several syncs are needed on the server.
   - Optional, owner-gated aid: read the stored NUMERIC features of the misjudged rows from `/var/lib/cabinet/snapshot.json` (numbers only).

D. Not a defect: 11 games are waiting for pictures because of the per-run cap (see above). The next release's verification must count games still waiting, not only image records by status.

E. Open item, out of scope for now: row 44 falls back to a main picture showing a different edition when the owned version's picture is a 3D shot. Note it for owner image overrides in a later phase.
