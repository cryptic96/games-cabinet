## Phase Goal

<objective>
Build the pixel side of the art work, test-first against synthetic pictures generated in code: the backdrop mask, the 3D-shot detector features, the art's main colour with plain backgrounds ignored, and the four edge colours that fill the bars around fitted art. One analysis pass per image, computed once during sync and stored later (D-11).

Purpose: IMG-01's flat-or-3D decision and CAB-03's spine colour both start from these measurements; synthetic fixtures make them reviewable and repeatable without any real image in the public repository (D-04 tuning happens later on the owner's sheet).
Output: `SyntheticArt` in the fake BGG project (shared with tests and, later, the fake image host), `ArtAnalysis`, `BackdropMask`, tests.
</objective>

3. `BackdropMask.Build(rgba, width, height)`: the ring is every pixel on the outer 1 px border; quantise each ring pixel to 4 bits per channel (a pixel with alpha below `TransparentAlpha` falls into one transparent bucket); keep buckets holding at least `RingClusterMinShare` of the ring; when the kept buckets together hold less than `BackdropMinRingShare` of the ring there is no backdrop (all false); otherwise compute each kept bucket's mean colour, seed every ring pixel within `BackdropTolerance` RGB distance of a kept mean (transparent matches transparent), and flood 4-connected into any pixel within the tolerance of any kept mean. Never use the drift-from-seed flood the research rejected.
4. `ArtAnalysis.Analyse(source)`: resize to `AnalysisWidth` wide (height by aspect, at least 1) as `Rgba8888`/`Unpremul` with linear sampling; build the mask; `BackdropShare` = masked / total; foreground = unmasked pixels with alpha at least `TransparentAlpha`; when there is no foreground, return fill 1, corners 0, sides 0; otherwise the bounding box of the foreground gives `Fill` (foreground count / box area), four corner squares of side `CornerSquareShare` of the shorter box side give the masked share of each, `Corner1` and `Corner2` are the two largest, and `SidesTouched` counts image sides the box touches. Main colour per D-11: the colour mask always holds transparent backdrop pixels; when the foreground is not rectangular (fill below `RectangularFill` or `Corner1` above `RectangularCorner`, a product shot) it holds every backdrop pixel, and when the foreground is rectangular (a flat cover) it holds only pixels of kept ring clusters whose mean has every channel at least `NearWhiteMin` (a light frame), so a solid-colour cover keeps its field colour (research Pitfall 5; these constants are fixed code, independent of the tunable verdict thresholds). Exclude the colour mask; when fewer than `MinForegroundShare` of pixels remain, the mean of opaque pixels (or `FallbackColour` when none); otherwise bucket the remaining opaque pixels at 4 bits per channel, score each bucket by the pixel count of its 3 by 3 by 3 neighbourhood times `(0.25 + 0.75 * mean HSV saturation of those pixels)`, break ties by the lowest bucket index (red * 256 + green * 16 + blue), and return the mean of the winning neighbourhood's pixels. Edge colours: per side a strip `max(MinEdgeStripPx, round(EdgeStripShare * dimension))` deep, alpha-weighted mean; a strip with fewer than half its pixels opaque takes the main colour.
5. `ArtAnalysisTests` (`[Trait("Category", "Images")]`), tracer cases: `BoxOnWhite` gives `ArtVerdict.ThreeD` with `ArtThresholds.Default`, a main colour with red at least 150 and green and blue at most 80, and four edge colours each with every channel at least 230; `FlatCover` gives `ArtVerdict.Flat`, backdrop share below 0.10 and a main colour whose red channel exceeds its green and blue channels.
