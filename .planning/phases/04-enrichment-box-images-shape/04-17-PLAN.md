<objective>
Close the three defects the owner found in the first server review round (`.continue-here.md` defects A, B and C, referred to by sheet row number only):

- A: real sizes whose shape matches a landscape flat cover stay portrait (row 49), because `BoxFromVersion.TryMap` always stands the longer side up and `BoxShape.Resolve` only reorients a front it rebuilt.
- B: clearly landscape pictures that are not judged flat never turn their box (rows 14, 25, 27, 44, 47), so they sit tall with big bars.
- C: the flat-versus-3D detector misjudges real pictures both ways: product shots on white or transparent backdrops read as flat with score 0.00 (rows 8, 9, 13, 28, 42; wrong picks on 8, 9, 13), and flat covers with dark or black edges read as 3D shots with score 1.00 (rows 1, 4, 25, 34).

Purpose: success criteria 3 and 4 (the image choice checked on the owner's editions; true proportions) failed on these rows; the second review round (plan 15, round 2) needs the fixes deployed (plan 18).
Output: turning rules in `BoxShape` with one new validated setting, a root-caused detector fix pinned by three new synthetic fixtures, a raised analysis version, updated operator docs.
</objective>
