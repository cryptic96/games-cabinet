## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Give every box its true proportions (IMG-03): real dimensions first, unless they clearly contradict a flat cover (D-07); otherwise a flat cover's shape; otherwise a stable estimate from the game's own details (D-08). Keep the decision of how a box stands independent of any image analysis by giving every item a verdict-free pose height (D-03).

Purpose: success criterion 4 ("real boxes in the cabinet visibly differ in size and shape") with no cabinet churn from small data drift or detector tuning.
Output: `BoxShape`, `SizeEstimate`, the pose height in the engine, two `Art` settings, tests and docs.
</objective>
