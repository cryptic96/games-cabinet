## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 4`): "Every game in the real cabinet shows its real box art (or a spine coloured from that art) at true proportions, with complete BGG details behind it, all served from the site itself."

<objective>
Let thin boxes look as thin as they really are (D-09): replace the two-line minimums with one-line floors, keep the two-line values as thresholds for a deterministic `showBaseLine` flag, and draw a lone title in the spine style below them. Fold in the box-look polish carried over from the layout prototype: whole-line steps on generated covers, the apron-shaped plinth opening, and the "+N more" accessible name that contains its visible text.

Purpose: true proportions (IMG-03) need thin boxes at their real thickness, and legible titles (CAB-03) need a one-line rule that never cuts text; the folded polish items close the layout prototype's taste notes before real art lands.
Output: engine floors and `showBaseLine` at layout version 10 with re-recorded goldens, renderer and strings changes, CSS polish, tests and docs.
</objective>
