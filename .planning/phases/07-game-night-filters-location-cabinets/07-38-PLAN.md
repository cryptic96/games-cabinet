# Plan 07-38: Geometry at every width, no top bar on a desktop, and the cabinet's columns follow the room while the panel pushes

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: amendment AM-3 (page frame measures), AM-6 ("Push" layout), AM-7 (the column rule, its worked values, where it lives, the guide), AM-14 (E11 overflow: no sideways scroll is checked in plan 07-43; the panel and rail scrolling inside on a short screen here) and AM-16 (geometry per breakpoint, no top bar, the column-rule row, the sections part of the push row, and the three `FilterBarGeometryTests` entries of the retire and replace table). Location cabinets (D-27) follow the room through the same basis.

<objective>
Make the pushed panel resize the cabinet the way the owner saw in the frames: the columns follow the room the cabinet has (two 32.25rem sections at 1440, two full sections at 1920), while folded and flying over the existing viewport rule stays. Pin the geometry of the rail and panel at every desktop width, retire the old bar geometry tests, and rewrite the layout guide's column paragraph.

Purpose: a pushed panel never leaves tiny boxes or one long column, and the layout guide tells the truth.
Output: the column rule in `cabinet.css`, `FilterDockGeometryTests` extended, `FilterBarGeometryTests` narrowed, `docs/cabinet-layout.md` rewritten.
</objective>
