# Plan 07-36: On a laptop the panel flies over the cabinet, every way to close it does one thing, and widths can change at any moment

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: amendment AM-2 (width changes), AM-3 (containing-block rule and its test), AM-6 (push and fly-over, keeping your place), AM-9 (modality, close routes, focus), AM-11 ("/" and the sheet's close from rotation, extended) and AM-16 (the focus, Escape and Back row; the push and fly-over row; the fixed-panel and press-outside parts of the flows row; and the three `FilterSheetTests` entries of the retire and replace table). It also carries the checker's extra width-crossing case in `FilterDockResizeTests`.

<objective>
Give the dock its behaviour: a modal fly-over between 64rem and 90rem with every close route of AM-9 doing exactly one thing, the focus moves of AM-9 in both states, a pushed panel that stays a plain neighbour and keeps the visitor's place when the cabinet resizes, and correct landings whenever the window crosses 64rem or 90rem. Prove the fly-over is never trapped by an ancestor containing block.

Purpose: the dock is safe for keyboard, screen-reader and Android users at every width before anything else is built on it.
Output: the behaviour in `filter-dock.js`, pure helpers with node tests, `FilterDockFocusTests`, `FilterDockResizeTests`, the containing-block test, the updated `FilterSheetTests`.
</objective>
