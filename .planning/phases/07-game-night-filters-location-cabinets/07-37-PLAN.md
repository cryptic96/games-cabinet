# Plan 07-37: The folded rail says what is filtered, the panel counts the games, and the phone's Filters button gets the three-line symbol

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: amendment AM-4 (rail state, count, Clear, names, disabled, no locations), AM-5 (the count line T3, the note), AM-10 (the phone Filters button: symbol, filled count, the 30rem rule), AM-12 (copy), AM-14 (E11 error, partial, long-text, zero-one-many) and AM-16 (the rail state row, the phone row, `node --test` copy vectors, and the four `FilterSlimBarTests` entries of the retire and replace table). U1 (one row while filtering) is kept and re-measured.

<objective>
Make the folded rail tell what is going on without opening anything (filled buttons with the value in their names, the two-line count, the icon Clear), give the panel its count line at rest, and put the three-line symbol and a filled count on the phone's Filters button so the same symbol means "filters" everywhere. All new text in both languages, pinned by the shared fixture.

Purpose: the owner's requirement keeps the options hidden by default; the rail and the phone button are how a visitor still sees what is filtered.
Output: rail and phone-button state in `filters.js`, new strings in `copy.js` and the fixture, the phone button's markup and style, `FilterDockRailTests`, updated `FilterSlimBarTests`.
</objective>
