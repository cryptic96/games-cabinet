# Plan 07-43: The rail and panel read on every wall at every hour, their rings stand outside, and visitors' settings are respected

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: amendment AM-13 (contrast pairs, the ring test, Test 3), AM-9 (rings, forced colours, reduced motion), AM-14 (E11 overflow: no sideways scroll), AM-16 (the access row, the AM-13 unit row, and the `FilterFocusRingTests` and `FilterAccessTests` entries of the retire and replace table). D-02 (the controls are wall chrome on every wall) carries over to the rail and panel.

<objective>

Purpose: the dock is as readable and as respectful of visitors' settings as the bar it replaces.
Output: extended `LookChromeContrastTests`, `filters-css.test.mjs`, `FilterFocusRingTests` and `FilterAccessTests`; token fixes only if a floor fails.
</objective>
