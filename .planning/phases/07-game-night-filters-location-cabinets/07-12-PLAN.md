## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01). This plan lifts UI E9 zero-one-many, long-text, loading, error, partial and overflow.

<objective>

Purpose: the owner can tell copies apart and place a whole shelf of copies in one move.
Output: edition labels, per-copy bulk moves and counts, the hint, tests.

| T-07-12-01 | Tampering (markup injection) | Edition name on owner pages | medium | mitigate | `CleanTitle` removed control and bidi characters at sync (plan 07-04); Razor encodes; `dir="auto"` on the tag |
