## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; the spec-less probe fallback ran. Edge probe: 23 applicable edges across the phase, 21 resolved explicit and lifted into the plans' truths, 2 unclassified (FILT-04, EXP-04) kept unresolved as flagged assumptions. Prohibition recall: 8 descriptor-less prohibitions across the plans, each disposing flagged-unverified. This plan lifts FILT-01 empty and encoding, FILT-05 boundary, empty, ordering and precision, UI E1 empty, E3 empty, and two prohibitions.

<objective>
Build the thinnest end-to-end path of the phase: a search field in a bar on the wall above the cabinet that, on every keystroke, puts every non-matching box in shadow in place (D-13, D-18), counts the matches the way the screen-reader list does (D-06, D-07), and clears with one tap (FILT-05). It proves the architecture every later plan builds on: the pure rules module, the veil above the light layer, the dim decided in the render pass (R5), the strict CSP, and the `cabinet:filtered` event other modules listen to. Then the field's keys, Clear and the debounced live region (D-08, D-09, D-20 R8).

Purpose: game night can start: "Lantern" lights the Lantern family and nothing else.
Output: `filter-rules.js`, `filters.js`, the veil, the bar, the strings the bar shows, `FilterSearchTests`. The rest of the phase's copy contract and the CI job for the filter browser tests follow in plan 07-06.
</objective>
