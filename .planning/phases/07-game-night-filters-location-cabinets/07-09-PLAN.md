# Plan 07-09: A copy that leaves and comes back keeps its place

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01). This plan lifts no probe edge.

<objective>
Move the owner-tools phase's 30-day rule to the per-copy key (D-31) and add the owner-accepted hand-over (research Q2): when the owner removes a game on BGG and adds it back, BGG gives a new collection entry, and the new copy takes over the kept choices of the absent one. Restores follow the same per-copy rule.

Purpose: fixing an edition on BGG never loses where a game lives.
Output: per-copy `OwnerExpiry.Reconcile` with the hand-over, its callers, the restore marking and preview count, tests.
</objective>
