# Plan 07-26: "Show on shelf" from the card

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01). This plan lifts UI E8 partial, error, long-text and loading.

<objective>
Turn the card's location row into a quiet "Show on shelf" link (D-30, I-A) that closes the card, switches to By location, scrolls the box into view (U4) and outlines it for two seconds (S6), reusing the view switch of plan 07-23, the scroll and the existing outline.

Purpose: "where is it?" is answered on the real shelf in one tap.
Output: the card button, the show-on-shelf flow in `locations-view.js`, the found outline, `FilterShowOnShelfTests`.
</objective>
