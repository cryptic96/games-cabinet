## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01). This plan lifts UI E1 partial, E5 loading and error, E6 loading and error.

Purpose: the owner standing at a shelf opens the site straight into "By location" next time, and the switch feels calm.
Output: remembered view, cross-fade, failure path, `scrollAfterSwitch`, `FilterViewSwitchTests`.
</objective>
