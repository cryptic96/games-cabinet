# Plan 07-02: A sparse last section stands short and complete

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01 for the totals). This plan lifts UI E10 overflow and E10 zero-one-many (backstop).

**Scope note (20 files, justified instead of split):** 13 of the 20 entries are the golden layout files and `layout-version.txt`, regenerated in one step by `CABINET_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` (a generated diff that is reviewed, not hand-edited), and 2 are the two paths of one `git mv` of the folded todo. The hand-written change is 5 files: the engine, two unit test classes, one browser test class and the layout guide. Splitting the goldens away from the engine change would leave the golden test red between plans, so they stay together.

<objective>
Make the last section of a cabinet as tall as the shelf rows that hold its games, with a minimum of two, so a cabinet that ends in one or two boxes reads as a short complete piece of furniture instead of empty shelves above a few boxes (D-32; folded todo "phone last section empty shelves"). It matters for this phase because every location cabinet has a last section (LOC-05). The change applies only to a sparse last section of an unbalanced build (owner-accepted research Q3), which covers every phone layout and every small location cabinet, and leaves balanced desktop sections and every non-sparse last section byte-identical to today.

Purpose: per-location cabinets will be small and many; each must look like furniture.
Output: the engine change, layout version 17, re-recorded goldens, the guide updated.
</objective>
