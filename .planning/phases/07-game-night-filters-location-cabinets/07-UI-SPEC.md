### One control set, two hosts

There is **one** set of the four group controls (view toggle, players, time, location). In full mode they sit in the bar. In compact mode they sit in the sheet, and the script moves the set between the two hosts when the width query changes (the sheet is closed first). Two sets are rejected: duplicate names and radios, and two states to keep in step. The search field and the count with Clear always stay in the bar.

### The bar's background and shadow (R1)

- **Unstuck** (in the page flow, right under the header): `background: transparent`. The wall and its soft falloff show through, so on Birch and Walnut (falloff 0.30) the bar has no visible edge against the falloff.
- **Stuck** (the sentinel has scrolled out above the viewport): the fill `var(--room)` **and** the shadow live together on **one `::after` layer** under the controls (`z-index: -1` inside the bar's stacking context), which appears by an **opacity fade** (`--swap-fade`, 120ms, instant under reduced motion), never by animating `box-shadow` or `background`. The bar itself stays transparent. The wall's falloff is drawn on `body` from the top of the page and scrolls with it, so the bar sticks while still over the brightest band on the two light walls; one fade avoids an instant darker band (S4):
  `box-shadow: 0 6px 12px -6px rgb(var(--room-shade-rgb) / 0.55), 0 1px 0 color-mix(in srgb, var(--room-line) 40%, transparent)`. The 1px hairline is part of the shadow list, not a border, so stuck and unstuck never differ in height by one pixel.
- **How the page knows:** an invisible zero-height marker element (`.filter-sentinel`, `aria-hidden`) sits directly before the bar. One `IntersectionObserver` (root: the viewport, `threshold: 0`) sets `data-stuck` on the bar when the marker's box is above the viewport top. No scroll handler.
- A flat `--room` bar over the falloff leaves a faint band right under the first stuck position on Birch and Walnut (the falloff is brighter just below the bar). The shadow separates the two. Judged in the screenshot round (ROUND R-3).
- On the Birch and Walnut walls the shadow tint is `--room-shade-rgb` (`60 52 44`), so it is a warm grey, not black.

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|

## Design lens: the cue list

The reference is **a real shelf in a room at night, with a few boxes under the lamp**. "Shadow" is the metaphor (D-18): a match stands in the light, everything else falls into shadow in place, and nothing disappears or moves. The cues that make this read as real and not as a UI filter:

| # | Cue of the real thing | Where it lives | Status |
|---|-----------------------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | One light, above and slightly left. Shadow falls the same way as every other shadow in the cabinet, and is tinted, never pure black | The veil uses `--box-shade-rgb` (`20 10 4`, the same tint as the shelf's shade) | FIXED |
| 2 | A box in shadow is still solid: it keeps its edge, its contact shadow and its colour (darkened), it does not become see-through | Veil is an overlay on an opaque box, plus the lit rim; no `opacity` on the box, except in forced colours (see "Dimmed box colours") | FIXED |
| 3 | Shadow lies over the box, but the lamp is behind it. A lit cubby never lifts a dimmed box | Veil is a child of the box (level 2); the light layer is at level 1 | FIXED |
| 4 | Pointing a torch at a box lifts its shadow | Hover peek at 0.20 on fine pointers | FIXED shape, START value |
| 5 | The room, not the furniture, carries the controls: a light switch panel on the wall, not a drawer front | The bar is wall chrome (`--room`), not wood, with no texture | FIXED |
| 6 | A label above each cabinet, like a sign on the wall | Location headings are page text in wall colours above each group | FIXED |

Weak points and how each is handled without a heavier effect:

- **A dimmed dark box merges with a dark back.** Computed body contrast of the veiled box against the back, weakest tone (Navy): brown 1.03, oak 1.06, walnut 1.06, mahogany 1.15, painted petrol 1.03. So the body does not carry the silhouette; the **1px lit rim does** (computed rim against the back, weakest: brown 1.97, oak 2.02, walnut 2.02, mahogany 2.20, painted 1.97). On Birch (light back) the veiled body itself separates (weakest Cream 1.81). The visibility test checks the rim, not the body.
- **Evening lights.** The veil is above the light layer for the box, so the lamp never washes a dimmed box back (browser test, see "Verification contract").
- **Many fades at once on a cheap phone.** A pass that changes more than 120 veils switches the fade off for that pass (instant). See "Motion and performance".

## The filter bar

### Where it is and how it is built (D-01, D-02)

```
<header> ... owner strip on the owner address ... </header>
<div class="filter-sentinel" aria-hidden="true"></div>
<div class="filter-bar" role="search" aria-label="Find a game" hidden>   (the script removes hidden before the first draw)
  row 1: [view toggle]  [search ............]  [players]                      full mode, two designed rows
  row 2: [time]  [location]                      [count + Clear]
  [search]  [Filters · 2]  [12 of 48]  [x]                                      compact mode, one row
</div>
<main> ... games list panel, #cabinet ... </main>
```

- The bar is a direct child of `<body>` between `<header>` and `<main>`, so `position: sticky` spans the whole page and, on the owner address, it sits **below the owner strip** (D-33).
- `position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 5`. Full bleed: `margin-inline: calc(-1 * var(--page-pad)); padding-inline: var(--page-pad)`. `margin-block-end: 16px` (md). `overflow: visible` so focus rings are never clipped.
- **Hidden entirely** (the `hidden` attribute) when there is nothing to filter: before the first sync, while the cabinet shows its filling or error message, and for an empty collection. It is shown in the same task that draws the first cabinet, so the cabinet never shifts under the reader.
- **Without script** the bar stays hidden and the existing no-script message applies.
- Tab order: skip link, header controls, the bar's controls in DOM order, then the cabinet's single tab stop. Each radio group is one tab stop (native), so the bar adds six stops at most.
- `html { scroll-padding-top: var(--filter-bar-h) }` and `.placement { scroll-margin-top: calc(var(--filter-bar-h) + 16px) }`, so a focused or scrolled-to box is never hidden under the bar. A `ResizeObserver` on the bar writes `--filter-bar-h` (the bar's full height, also while the slim bar is slid away).

### Full mode: 64rem and wider (**ASSUMPTION A1**)

DOM and visual order (identical): **view toggle, search, players, time, location, count and Clear**.

| Part | Spec |
|------|------|

## Filter rules (the matching contract) (D-10 to D-17, D-24)

All matching is a pure function in `filter-rules.js`, covered by `node --test` in `build/tests/`. The page runs it in the same pass that draws the boxes.

| Rule | Contract |
|------|----------|
| Combination | Search, players, time and location combine with **AND** |
| Family | A base game and its owned expansions. An orphan expansion is a family of one. An expansion owned for several bases is lit when **any** family it belongs to is lit |
| Game-night filters keep the family together | Search, players and time are decided **per family**; every box of a lit family is lit (EXP-04) |
| Location is decided per box | A box is judged by its **own** location (D-24). An expansion stored apart lights while its base dims, and the reverse, when only location differs. An expansion that follows its base has the base's location |
| Search normalisation | Trim; Unicode NFD, drop combining marks, lower-case, collapse whitespace; "cafe" finds "Cafe" and the accented title. The query is one substring (A26). Titles are displayed exactly as BGG gives them; normalising for matching is not modifying data |
| Search target | The base title, every owned expansion's title, and every BGG alternate title of those (D-14). The alternate-name mechanism is a research choice; this contract only fixes behaviour: title matches appear **in the same frame as the keystroke**; alternate-title matches may join when the index arrives, with no spinner and no wrong flash (the count updates quietly), and nothing shows why an alternate title matched (**A17**) |
| Players | Family range = smallest known minimum to largest known maximum across the base and its owned expansions; 0 or missing values are ignored. Lit when `min <= n <= max`; **8+** is lit when `max >= 8`. The card keeps showing the base game's own BGG range |
| Time | A match when `0 < playingTime <= chosen`, using the **base game's own** stated playing time (BGG's long end). A 45 to 90 minute game does not light at 60. Expansions do not shorten it |
| Unknown dims | A family with no usable minimum **or** no usable maximum dims while players is set (**A10**). A game with playing time 0 or missing dims while time is set. Only confirmed matches light |
| Location values | Any, a location id, or Unknown (no location). Stored by id, never by name |
| Count | See "Count, Clear and the live region" |
| Zero matches | Every box dims, the count says "No games match", Clear is highlighted. No note covers the cabinet |
| Stored filter no longer valid | A stored location id that no longer exists (deleted or emptied) falls back to Any. A stored view of "By location" with no locations falls back to "All together" without overwriting the remembered choice |
| Card data needed | Players, time and location need the card data. Search needs only the layout's names and family links. If a **stored** filter other than search is active at load, the first draw waits for the card data so no undimmed frame appears (**A5**). If the card data fails, players, time and location are disabled (disabled look, see "Color") with the quiet note "Couldn't load the details. Reload the page to try again." (the existing string) and the stored filters other than search are not applied |

### Persistence (D-17, D-28)

- **Filters:** `sessionStorage`, one fixed key, a small JSON object (`q`, `players`, `time`, `location`). Per tab. Survives reload, the EN · NL switch (a navigation in the same tab) and a live redraw. A new tab or a later visit starts clean. Never in a cookie, never in the address. Writes are coalesced (250ms).
- **View:** `localStorage`, one fixed key, the string `all` or `location`. Read **before** the layout is fetched so the wrong cabinet never flashes. No cookie, nothing in the address. A first visit is "All together".
- If a storage call throws (private mode), the page keeps the state in memory and carries on; nothing breaks.

## The shadow (dim) look (D-18 to D-20, R5 to R8)

### Structure (FIXED)

- Every box carries `<span class="placement-veil" aria-hidden="true"></span>` as its **last child**, created by `buildPlacement` in `render.js` on every draw (a pass that finds no filter active still creates it, so a later change only toggles an attribute). Cost: **one extra empty node per box**, about 400 on the 400 sample. If the phone check shows a cost, the planner may create the veil lazily when the first filter becomes active; the contract (above-light stacking, no undimmed frame) does not change.
- **Dimming is decided while drawing** (R5): the filter result goes into the same render pass; a box that is dim gets `data-dim` before it is inserted. A reload, a language switch, a live redraw, a view switch and the remembered view never show an undimmed frame first. Filter changes afterwards only toggle `data-dim` on existing boxes.
- `data-dim` is on the **box** (`.placement`). A `moreMarker` ("+N more") dims with the family it belongs to.
- **Dimmed means dimmed, not disabled** (D-20): a dimmed box keeps `cursor: pointer`, stays a button, keeps its full `aria-label`, and a click or tap opens its card at full contrast as usual. No `aria-disabled`. The card never carries the veil.
- **Stacking:** the veil is a positioned child of the box (`position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 1`), so it paints above the box's own label and art and, because the box is level 2 and the section light is level 1, **above the light layer for that box**. The box's corner tag on the owner address is raised to `z-index: 2` so the "not placed" tag stays visible on a dimmed box. The focus ring and the open outline are the box's `outline`, drawn outside the veil.

### Skeleton (modifiers layer; STARTING VALUES, strict-CSP safe)

```css
:root { --veil-alpha: 0.6; --veil-peek-alpha: 0.2; --veil-fade: 160ms; }
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { :root { --veil-fade: 0ms; } }

.placement-veil {
  position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 1; pointer-events: none; opacity: 0;
  background: rgb(var(--box-shade-rgb) / var(--veil-alpha));
  box-shadow: inset 1px 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.2);
  transition: opacity var(--veil-fade) ease-out;
}
.placement[data-dim] > .placement-veil { opacity: 1; }
.placement[data-found] > .placement-veil { opacity: 0; }
@media (hover: hover) {
  .placement[data-dim]:hover > .placement-veil { opacity: calc(var(--veil-peek-alpha) / var(--veil-alpha)); }
}
@media (forced-colors: active) { .placement[data-dim] { opacity: 0.5; } }
```

Only the veil's opacity animates. No blur, no grey filter, no opacity on the box, except the static `opacity: 0.5` in forced colours, where the system drops the veil's fill. `data-bulk` on `#cabinet` (set by the script for one pass that changes more than 120 veils) sets `--veil-fade: 0ms` for that pass.

### Reading a dimmed title (D-19, R7)

A dimmed label is **deliberately under 4.5:1**. Computed at the START strength 0.6, over the 12 palette tones (label colour and box colour both under the veil): **2.17 to 3.14:1** (Rose 2.17, Sage 2.22, Mustard 2.23, Sky 2.32, Rust 2.48, Teal 2.56, Slate 2.93, Forest 2.93, Oxblood 3.00, Plum 3.02, Cream 3.05, Navy 3.14). Mitigations, all FIXED:

1. every box keeps its full accessible name and tooltip;
2. the screen-reader list carries the matches, and the filtered list is also reachable by sighted keyboard users through the skip link;
3. a dimmed box opens its card at full contrast;
4. on desktop, pointing at a dimmed box lifts the veil to 0.20 (the peek). Computed label contrast at the peek, worst case (Rust, in the band under the shelf's own shade at the default Shaded depth, where the two shades stack to 0.36): **3.76:1**; with no shelf shade it is 4.51:1; at Flat depth 4.51:1; at Deep 4.08:1. The peek is for reading a title before clicking, not a 4.5:1 surface. Instant under reduced motion.

### Keyboard, focus and announcements while filtering (D-20, R8)

- **Arrows move only between matches.** Tab enters the cabinet on the first match (the cabinet's single tab stop moves to the first lit box). Clearing the filters restores normal arrow navigation. With **zero** matches the arrows run over all boxes as usual, so a keyboard user is never trapped (**A27**).
- If the box that holds the cabinet's single tab stop becomes dimmed, the tab stop moves to the **nearest match by on-screen position** (reuse `keys.js`'s rectangle scoring). If the dimmed box has focus right now, focus stays on it; only the tab stop moves.
- Home and End go to the first and last **match**, in the same on-screen order.
- Enter or Space on any focused box (dimmed or not) opens the card, as today.
- **"/"** pressed anywhere outside a text field, a select or an open dialog focuses the search and selects its text (I-B). A modifier key combination is ignored. The hint shows only on hover-capable devices.
- **Escape in the search field** with text clears it (and prevents the default); with no text it does nothing special. **Enter** blurs the field on a coarse pointer so the keyboard closes; on a fine pointer focus stays.
- The count and view announcements follow "Count, Clear and the live region".

### Sparse last section (D-32)

The last section of a cabinet (and so of every location cabinet) is drawn **only as tall as its filled shelf rows, with a minimum of two**, and its boxes stand on the bottom shelf. Top, sides and base follow the shorter height. It reads as a short, complete piece of furniture, never as empty shelves above a few boxes.

- Visual rules: no empty shelf rows above the boxes; the section's `--section-h` is the engine's new value, so the reserved `aspect-ratio` and every design layer follow without a CSS change; feet still share the row's bottom line (sections stand on the bottom of their row, as today).
- **The default look must stay pixel for pixel as before for every collection that does not end in a sparse last section.** Only the layout version changes; golden layouts are re-recorded, and a synthetic collection ending in one or two boxes at phone width proves the fix. Desktop balancing and section heights elsewhere are unchanged (asserted).
- `docs/cabinet-layout.md` is updated in plain language (what the last section does, how location cabinets are built), with no planning references.

### "Show on shelf" from the card (D-30, I-A)

- The card's `Stored in` row keeps its label (14px 600 `--paper-label`) and the **value becomes a quiet button**: `<button type="button" class="card-where-link">` with the location name (16px 400, `--ink`, underlined, `text-underline-offset: 3px`) and a 14px `#i-chev` after the text, 4px away, inside the line so a long name wraps over whole rulings. No fill, no border, `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`, the card's ink focus ring. It follows the card's **Tap rows** rule: the row is `calc(var(--hit-rows) * var(--ruling))` tall (two rulings on coarse pointers), hover and active wash `rgb(42 26 16 / 0.06)` like `.card-link a`. Accessible name: "Show on shelf: Shelf B" (the visible text is contained).
- **Action:** it closes the card (the normal close, the box flies back), then switches to "By location" if needed (the cross-fade above), **scrolls so the box is in view** (U4, FIXED): if the location's heading and the box both fit in the viewport below the bar, the heading is aligned under the bar (`scrollIntoView` on the group with `block: 'start'`, honouring `scroll-margin-top`); otherwise the box is centred (`scrollIntoView` on the box with `block: 'center'`). The scroll is instant. It then **briefly outlines the box** with `data-found` for **2000ms** (`FOUND_HOLD_MS`, START). `data-found` reuses the existing open outline (`2px solid var(--room-text)`, offset 2px, level 4) and **lifts that box's veil** while it is set, so a filter never hides the answer. It does not clear any filter. The outline is static, with or without reduced motion; it also clears on the next `pointerdown` or `keydown`, never on pointer movement, so a moved mouse does not clear it at once (S6).
- An expansion with no location of its own that follows its base shows the base's location and scrolls to the expansion's own box in that cabinet.
- A game with no location has no `Stored in` row, so no link.
- If the "By location" payload fails to load, the failure copy appears (see below), the view stays "All together" and no outline is set.

### By location failure

The switch keeps the current view on screen, and on a failed or invalid payload it shows the polite message "Couldn't load the location view. Showing everything together." in `#filter-live`, sets the toggle back to "All together" and **does not** overwrite the remembered view. A retry is the next switch.

## Live redraw, view and sheet (discretion resolved)

- A live redraw after a sync or an owner change **keeps** the view, the filters, the sheet (if open) and the scroll position. The render pass takes the current filter state, so the redraw is dimmed correctly in the first frame. The existing rule that a redraw waits for an open card to close is unchanged. An open Filters sheet does **not** hold a redraw back (the sheet's backdrop is transparent, so the visitor sees it); the sheet's "Show {n} games" label and the count update in the same task.
- If the owner's change makes the stored location filter invalid, it falls back to Any and the count updates; if the owner has no locations any more, the view toggle and the location control disappear and the view returns to "All together" (not remembered).
- A look change (06.1) is independent: the bar and the sheet read wall tokens and follow it. If the wall tone changes between light and dark, the bar and sheet colours follow through the same tokens, with the existing look cross-fade.
- A change in the profile (phone or desktop, 40rem) reloads the layout as today; filters stay (state, not layout). A change across 64rem changes only the bar mode.

These were not answered by the decisions or the review. Each takes the option most consistent with the earlier contracts and the senior-frontend skill.

| # | Assumption | Why |
|---|------------|-----|
| A1 | The slim bar and sheet apply **below 64rem**, not only at the phone width (40rem). From 40rem to 64rem the sheet is bottom-anchored, `min(100%, 40rem)` wide and centred. The full bar needs about 976px for two rows | The full bar does not fit a tablet in fewer than three rows. The layout profile and the card sheet stay at 40rem |
| A2 | The Filters sheet is capped at **70dvh** (the card sheet is 90dvh) and scrolls inside beyond that | The cabinet must stay visible above it |
| A3 | The sheet is **wall chrome** (`--room`, `--room-text`), not paper | Paper is for objects (the card, owner pages); the bar and sheet carry the same controls and one stylesheet serves both |
| A4 | The selected chip, the selected segment, the primary sheet button and the highlighted Clear use the **inverted wall colours**, never accent | The accent reserved list is fixed |
| A5 | When a stored filter other than search is active at load, the **first draw waits for the card data** | The decision requires no undimmed frame; players, time and location come from the card data. Without a stored filter the existing order (layout first, cards after) is unchanged |
| A6 | The sheet has its **own Clear**, and its primary button reads **"Close"** when nothing matches | The bar is inert while the modal sheet is open; "Show 0 games" is not a useful label |
| A7 | The veil is an **extra child node** on every box, drawn with a lit rim on the veil | Boxes already use both pseudo-elements, a veil must cover label and art, and an inset shadow cannot cover content |
| A8 | The **peek** is 0.20 and its worst case is 3.76:1, not 4.5:1 | At the default Shaded depth the shelf shade already stacks to 0.36; 4.5 is reached only at Flat depth or off the shaded band |
| A9 | The veil fade is 160ms and is **dropped for a pass that changes more than 120 veils** | Many simultaneous opacity layers on a cheap phone |
| A10 | "Unknown" players means **no usable minimum or no usable maximum** for the family | A confirmed answer needs both ends |
| A11 | A lit entry counts when **at least one of its own boxes is lit**; in "All together" an entry is a family, in "By location" an expansion stored apart is its own entry | Keeps the count equal to the list the screen-reader user hears |
| A12 | While filtering, a location heading's count reads **"3 of 14 games"** | Useful at the shelf: it says which cabinet holds the matches |
| A13 | Location headings are **centred** over their group, 24px name and 16px muted count | Centred rows of one to three sections need a heading that does not depend on the first column |
| A14 | The "stored in" phrase in the list is **omitted entirely** while the owner has no locations | Otherwise every entry says "unknown location" |
| A15 | The search field's **native cancel button is removed** | It cannot follow the wall colour; Escape and Clear exist |
| A16 | **Enter blurs the field only on a coarse pointer** | On a desktop it would drop keyboard focus for nothing |

| A18 | "Show on shelf" uses the existing **chevron icon**, not an arrow character, and does not clear filters | The sprite already carries the chevron the expansion rows use |
