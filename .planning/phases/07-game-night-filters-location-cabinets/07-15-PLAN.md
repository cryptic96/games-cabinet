# Plan 07-15: The desktop bar stays put - two quiet rows, pinned, with a shadow only once it is stuck

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: no SPEC; spec-less probe fallback ran (see plan 07-01). This plan lifts UI E1 overflow.

<objective>
Give the desktop bar its designed shape (D-01, D-02, U2, U3): pinned at the top while the cabinet scrolls, transparent in the page flow, a wall-coloured fill and shadow only once stuck (detected without a scroll handler), two designed rows at a steady 112 px from 64rem, and a bar height the page uses so focus and scrolling never hide a box under the bar.

Purpose: the bar is always in reach while browsing a long cabinet, and filtering never makes it jump.
Output: the pinned two-row bar, `--page-pad`, `--room-wash`, `--filter-bar-h`, `FilterBarGeometryTests`.
</objective>
