# Plan 07-40: Show on shelf, the view switch and live redraws meet the dock; two flaky tests are hardened

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (not yet in user-story form; flagged, see `/gsd mvp-phase 7`): "A visitor can narrow the cabinet to tonight's game by dimming everything else, and can switch between one big cabinet and one cabinet per storage location."

Coverage note: amendment AM-11 (Show on shelf, live redraw, scroll padding, the view switch and K2, card over a pushed panel) and AM-16 (the flows row, and the `FilterShowOnShelfTests` and `FilterViewSwitchTests` part of the last row of the retire and replace table). It also takes in two items of `.planning/todos/pending/2026-10-17-harden-browser-tests-that-flake-on-ci.md` (the Show on shelf landing and the WebKit live-look test, the two that went red on `main`), because both run in the release's checks and the first is touched here anyway; the rest of that todo stays open.

<objective>
Make the flows that cross the dock land right: Show on shelf beside a pushed panel or the rail, the view switch's fade under an open panel and its upward scroll, and live redraws that keep the dock as it is. Move the desktop cases of the two flow test classes to the panel, and harden the two browser tests that failed on the runner after the last merge.

Purpose: the features visitors already use keep working beside the new dock, and the release's checks stop flaking on these two tests.
Output: `locations-view.js` and `card.css` changes, `FilterDockFlowTests`, migrated and hardened `FilterShowOnShelfTests`, migrated `FilterViewSwitchTests`, a hardened `LiveLookWebKitTests`, the todo updated.
</objective>
